## Number Of Data Centers, Their Locations, Gpus, And Power Capacity

**Nscale-operated data centers:**
- **Glomfjord, Norway** – Located in the Arctic Circle. 100% renewable hydropower. 30MW operational capacity, expandable to 60MW. Uses adiabatic cooling and direct-to-chip liquid cooling. AMD MI250X GPU cluster (Svartisen Cluster, made Top500 list). Each node: 4x AMD Instinct MI250X OAMs (8x GPUs), 2x AMD EPYC 7713 CPUs.
- **Narvik, Norway** – Stargate Norway, in partnership with Aker ASA and OpenAI. 230MW initial capacity with plans for additional 290MW expansion. Powered by renewable hydropower. Target: 100,000 NVIDIA GPUs.
- **Loughton, United Kingdom** – London-proximate AI campus. Power allocation scalable up to 90MW. Direct-liquid cooling, waste heat reuse.
- **Ward County, Texas, USA** – ~240MW AI data center, developed with Ionic Digital. Plans to expand to 1.2GW. Closed-loop direct-liquid cooling and rear-door heat exchangers.

**Partner-run data centers:**
- **Sines, Portugal** – At the Start Campus data center on Portugal's Atlantic coast. Designed for GW-scale AI deployments. 100% renewable energy with seawater cooling.
- **Keflavik, Iceland** – One of Iceland's largest liquid-cooled GPU installations. Set to host more than 4,600 NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs across Verne's Icelandic campus in 2026. 100% renewable (geothermal and hydropower).
