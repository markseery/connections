# Teraflops and Exaflops: Consolidated Summary

## Supercomputer Rankings & Performance

## Training Compute Requirements

- An LLM with **175 billion parameters** requires approximately **3.14 × 10²³ FLOPs** for full training.
- **Llama 3.1 405B** training referenced as an example of extreme compute demands at scale.

## MLPerf Benchmark Results

### Training (v5.0 / v5.1)
- **Llama 3.1 405B training step**: **124.5 min** on a 128-node (1,024 GPU) cluster; **244.6 min** on a 64-node (512 GPU) cluster — demonstrating **near-linear scaling (1.97×)**.
- MLPerf Training v5.1: NVIDIA HGX B300 and HGX B200 systems captured **7 out of 9 first-place results**.

### Inference (v5.1)
- **GB200 NVL72**: **855.82 tokens/s** for Llama 3.1 405B in offline mode.
- **8× HGX B200 systems**: Up to **245,000 tokens/s** total throughput.
- NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 and HGX B200 led overall inference results.

## Hardware Specifications

- **NVIDIA GB200 NVL72**: **130 TB/s** GPU-to-GPU communication via fifth-generation NVLink.
- **NVIDIA B200 GPU**: **180 GB** GPU memory; **3.2 Tbit/s** InfiniBand connectivity.

## Real-World Inference Throughput (Production Systems)

- **Token Factory (DeepSeek R1 0528)**: **248 output tokens/s** — top-2 throughput worldwide per Artificial Analysis.
- **Eigen AI (GPT-OSS-120B)**: Up to **911 output tokens/s**.

## Key Takeaways

- Modern supercomputing benchmarks are increasingly framed around **LLM training and inference** rather than traditional floating-point peaks alone.
- Near-linear scaling (1.97× for 2× the nodes) demonstrates effective hardware utilization at the multi-thousand-GPU level.
- No specific **exaflop-class** system performance figures were cited in either batch, though the compute budgets for frontier LLM training (10²³+ FLOPs) inherently require sustained exascale-level operations over extended training runs.
