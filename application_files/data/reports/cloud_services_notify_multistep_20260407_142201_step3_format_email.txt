<!-- Financing & Debt -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:28px 40px 0;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:15px;font-weight:700;color:#3b82f6;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.5px;">Financing &amp; Debt</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">
CoreWeave closed an <strong>$8.5 billion Delayed Draw Term Loan (DDTL 4.0)</strong> backed by GPUs and a long-term Meta contract. The new facility carries a significantly lower interest rate (SOFR + 2.25% vs. 4.00% previously) and shifts from asset-based borrowing limits to an execution-based, project-style model incorporating power availability. The stock jumped ~12% on the news. However, CoreWeave's total debt now exceeds <strong>$21 billion</strong> (up from ~$8B in 2024), with quarterly interest expenses of $388 million consuming nearly a third of revenue.
</p>
</td>
</tr>

<!-- Revenue & Growth -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:28px 40px 0;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:15px;font-weight:700;color:#3b82f6;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.5px;">Revenue &amp; Growth</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">
CoreWeave reported <strong>110% YoY revenue growth</strong> in Q4 2025, with full-year 2025 revenue hitting <strong>$5.1 billion</strong> (up 168%). Its contractual backlog surged 300%+ to <strong>$66.8 billion</strong>. Management forecasts 2026 revenue of $12–13 billion. Despite top-line growth, the company operated at a loss for Q4 and the full year, with plans to spend up to <strong>$35 billion in 2026</strong> on infrastructure buildout. Analysts expect profitability within five years.
</p>
</td>
</tr>

<!-- Poolside Deal Collapse -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:28px 40px 0;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:15px;font-weight:700;color:#3b82f6;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.5px;">Poolside Deal Collapse</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">
CoreWeave and AI startup <strong>Poolside</strong> mutually terminated their partnership on <strong>Project Horizon</strong>, a planned 2 GW data center campus in West Texas. CoreWeave's new $8.5B loan gave it flexibility to shift away from fixed single-tenant commitments toward multi-partner leasing. Poolside is now seeking alternative partners, including reportedly <strong>Google</strong>, and also lost a planned Nvidia-backed $2 billion funding round.
</p>
</td>
</tr>

<!-- Applied Digital Lease Deal -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:28px 40px 0;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:15px;font-weight:700;color:#3b82f6;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.5px;">Applied Digital Lease Deal</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">
CoreWeave amended an existing data center lease with <strong>Applied Digital</strong> (APLD), suspending terms on two of four data halls and entering a new lease covering those halls through a CoreWeave subsidiary, sending Applied Digital stock higher.
</p>
</td>
</tr>

<!-- Customer Wins -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:28px 40px 0;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:15px;font-weight:700;color:#3b82f6;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.5px;">Customer Wins</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">
CoreWeave was selected by <strong>Zonos</strong> to power cross-border commerce solutions and by <strong>Cline</strong> to power autonomous engineering workloads. The company is now emphasizing <strong>inference</strong> alongside training, and earned SemiAnalysis' <strong>Platinum ClusterMAX rating</strong> twice.
</p>
</td>
</tr>
