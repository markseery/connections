<!-- OpenAI–Microsoft -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0a0a0a;letter-spacing:-0.3px;">OpenAI–Microsoft Partnership Restructured</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
OpenAI and <strong>Microsoft</strong> renegotiated their partnership, replacing Microsoft's exclusive access to OpenAI IP with a <strong>nonexclusive license through 2032</strong>. Microsoft remains OpenAI's "primary cloud partner," and OpenAI committed to buying $250B more of <strong>Azure</strong> cloud services. Critically, <strong>OpenAI can now serve products across any cloud provider</strong>, resolving potential legal conflict stemming from OpenAI's up-to-$50B deal with <strong>Amazon</strong>. Products will still ship "first on Azure" unless Microsoft cannot support the required capabilities.
</p>
