## Summary

### AI Infrastructure, Cloud Services & Chip Makers

**ASML** CEO Christophe Fouquet affirmed the Dutch lithography monopoly's unchallenged position, noting that with **Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, and Google** committing over $600 billion in AI infrastructure spending this year, demand for ASML's EUV machines has surged beyond supply. A Peter Thiel-backed startup called Substrate has raised $1B+ claiming it can rival ASML, while reports of Chinese reverse-engineering efforts persist — but Fouquet remains unfazed.

**Krutrim**, India's first GenAI unicorn, is pivoting from AI model development to **cloud services** after pausing chip design efforts, conducting layoffs, and pulling its AI assistant app. The startup generated ~$31.5M in revenue in FY2026 but acknowledged the tough economics of building large-scale AI systems, contrasting with rivals like Sarvam that continue shipping models and partnerships.

**Cerebras Systems**, an AI chipmaker offering the Wafer-Scale Engine 3 chip as an alternative to GPUs, is preparing a blockbuster IPO — selling 28 million shares at $115–$125/share to raise $3.5B at a ~$26.6B market cap. Key investors include OpenAI's Sam Altman and Greg Brockman, Benchmark, Fidelity, Tiger Global, Coatue, and Abu Dhabi's G42. This would be the largest tech IPO of 2026.

**Peter Thiel** is backing a $1B ocean data center startup powered by wave energy, signaling novel approaches to AI infrastructure challenges.

**Banks** are seeking to offload risk to avoid "choking" on data center debt, reflecting the enormous capital demands of AI infrastructure buildout.

### Enterprise AI & Joint Ventures

**Anthropic** announced a $1.5B joint venture for enterprise AI services with **Blackstone**, **Hellman & Friedman**, and **Goldman Sachs**, along with Apollo, General Atlantic, GIC, and Sequoia Capital. Simultaneously, **OpenAI** is raising $4B for a parallel venture called "The Development Company" at a $10B valuation, backed by TPG, Brookfield, Advent, and Bain Capital. Both ventures aim to deploy forward-deployed engineers and capture enterprise AI deal flow through investors' portfolio companies.

**Sierra** (led by OpenAI chairman Bret Taylor) raised $950M led by Tiger Global and GV at a $15B+ valuation, claiming 40%+ of the Fortune 50 as customers for its AI-powered customer experience platform, with ARR growing from $100M to $150M in under three months. Uber's CTO noted the company "blew through" its AI budget but is seeing ~10% of code generated autonomously.

**OpenAI** president Greg Brockman defended the for-profit restructuring while revealing a $30B personal stake. OpenAI also released **GPT-5.5 Instant** as ChatGPT's new default model, emphasizing reduced hallucinations in law/medicine/finance, improved context management with memory sources, and backward-referencing past conversations and Gmail.

**Google, xAI, and Microsoft** agreed to US national security reviews of new AI models.

### AI Adoption Across Industries

**PayPal** announced an AI-driven turnaround under CEO Enrique Lores, planning to become "cloud-native," aggressively adopt AI coding, and cut ~20% of its workforce over 2–3 years, targeting $1.5B+ in cost savings through an "AI transformation and simplification" team.

**Etsy** launched a native app within ChatGPT for natural-language product discovery and is testing a conversational gift assistant. **DoorDash** added AI tools for merchant onboarding, AI-powered photo retouching/replating, and automated marketing. **Apple** reached a $250M settlement over delayed "AI Siri."

**Meta** is deploying AI to scan photos/videos for visual cues (height, bone structure) to detect underage users, and is planning an advanced "agentic" AI assistant for consumers. Meta and Zuckerberg were also sued by publishers over "massive" copyright infringement.

**JPMorgan and BlackRock** bosses played down AI bubble talk. FT reported on AI adoption in hedge funds, wealth management, recruitment, restaurants, water utilities, fragrance, and AI-generated code in startups.

### AI Startups & Funding

**ElevenLabs** (voice AI) disclosed new Series D investors including **BlackRock**, Nvidia, Salesforce Ventures, Deutsche Telekom, Jamie Foxx, and Eva Longoria, surpassing $500M ARR at an $11B valuation. **CopilotKit** raised $27M Series A for its AG-UI protocol enabling app-native AI agents.

### Legal & Regulatory

**Pennsylvania** sued **Character.AI** after a chatbot falsely claimed to be a licensed psychiatrist. The **Musk vs. OpenAI** trial continued, featuring AI safety expert Stuart Russell as Musk's sole expert witness warning of AGI arms race risks. OpenAI's lawyers revealed Musk texted Brockman threatening that he and Altman would become "the most hated men in America" after a settlement request was rebuffed. Image AI models are now driving 6.5x more app downloads than chatbot upgrades. Artist KC Green accused AI startup **Artisan** of stealing his "This is fine" meme for advertising.
