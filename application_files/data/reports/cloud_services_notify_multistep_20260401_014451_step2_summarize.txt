# Summary of All Items

## OpenAI's Major Moves

**OpenAI closed a record $122 billion funding round** at an $852 billion valuation, co-led by SoftBank, Andreessen Horowitz, D.E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, and T. Rowe Price, with participation from **Amazon, Nvidia, and Microsoft**. About $3 billion came from retail investors, and the company will be included in ARK Invest ETFs ahead of an expected IPO. OpenAI claims $2 billion/month in revenue, 900 million weekly active users, 50 million subscribers, and an ads pilot generating over $100 million ARR in under six weeks. The company expanded its revolving credit facility to $4.7 billion.

**OpenAI shut down Sora**, its AI video-generation tool, just six months after launch. User count had collapsed to under 500,000 while burning ~$1 million/day in compute costs. The decision was driven by a strategic refocus on enterprise/developer tools ahead of a possible IPO — partly in response to **Anthropic's Claude Code** gaining momentum. **Disney's planned $1 billion partnership** tied to Sora collapsed; Disney was reportedly blindsided, learning of the shutdown less than an hour before the public announcement. No money had changed hands.

**OpenAI added plugin support to Codex**, its agentic coding tool, enabling integrations with GitHub, Gmail, Cloudflare, Vercel, and others — catching up to similar features already available in Claude Code. OpenAI also **indefinitely shelved plans for an erotic ChatGPT "adult mode"** after backlash from investors, staff, and advisors who warned it could create a "sexy suicide coach."

## Anthropic's Turbulent Month

**Anthropic accidentally leaked the full source code of Claude Code** — nearly 2,000 TypeScript files and 512,000+ lines of code — when version 2.1.88 of its npm package included a source map file. Security researcher Chaofan Shou discovered it immediately. Anthropic called it "a release packaging issue caused by human error, not a security breach." This came just days after Anthropic accidentally made ~3,000 internal files publicly available, including details of an unannounced model. The leak revealed Claude Code as "a production-grade developer experience, not just a wrapper around an API."

**Claude Code gained new computer-control capabilities**, allowing it to point, click, and navigate users' screens to complete tasks — available to Pro and Max subscribers on MacOS as a "research preview." Anthropic is also **battling the Department of Defense** over AI use, with the Trump administration blocked from punishing Anthropic over the Pentagon dispute. **Google** is reportedly nearing a deal to help finance a multibillion-dollar data center leased to Anthropic.

Anthropic published a report on AI's labor market impact, claiming LLMs could theoretically perform 80%+ of tasks across many job categories, though the underlying methodology relied on speculative 2023-era assumptions.

## Cloud Infrastructure & Data Centers

**Mistral AI raised $830 million in debt** to build an Nvidia-powered data center near Paris (operational Q2 2026) and announced $1.4 billion in Swedish AI infrastructure investment, aiming to deploy 200MW of compute across Europe by 2027. CEO Arthur Mensch emphasized reducing dependence on third-party cloud providers.

**Starcloud raised $170 million (Series A)** at a $1.1 billion valuation to build data centers in space, backed by Benchmark and EQT Ventures. It launched its first satellite with an **Nvidia H100 GPU** in November 2025 and plans a version with **Nvidia Blackwell** chips and an **AWS server blade**. Cost competitiveness depends on SpaceX's Starship achieving frequent commercial launches (potentially 2028-2029).

**ScaleOps raised $130 million** at an $800 million valuation for software that automatically manages and reallocates computing resources (GPUs, memory, storage) in real time, claiming up to 80% reduction in cloud and AI infrastructure costs. The founder previously worked at **Run:ai** (acquired by **Nvidia**).

**AI chip startup Rebellions** (South Korea) raised $400 million in a pre-IPO round at a $2.34 billion valuation, bringing total funding to $850 million. It launched RebelRack and RebelPOD inference infrastructure platforms and is expanding to the U.S., Japan, Saudi Arabia, and Taiwan, targeting **cloud providers, neoclouds**, government agencies, and telecoms.

**Nvidia invested $2 billion in chipmaker Marvell** to boost AI networking. UK chip startup **Fractile** is seeking to raise $200 million to challenge Nvidia.

**U.S. Senators Warren and Hawley** urged the Energy Information Administration to mandate annual electricity-use disclosures from data centers, amid growing consumer concerns about energy costs. 65% of Americans oppose building AI data centers in their communities. The **FT** asked whether the AI data center boom could become a "$9 trillion bust," and a top energy developer warned about overbuilding power supplies for AI.

## Salesforce / Slack AI Overhaul

**Salesforce unveiled 30 new AI features for Slack**, highlighted by reusable AI-skills for Slackbot, MCP (Model Context Protocol) client capability enabling connection to external services including Salesforce's Agentforce platform, and meeting transcription/summarization.

## Amazon / Ring / Alexa

**Amazon's Alexa+** added conversational food ordering from Uber Eats and Grubhub, rolling out on Echo Show devices. **Ring launched an app store** leveraging its 100 million+ cameras for AI-powered use cases in elder care (SoftBank-backed Density), queue management (QueueFlow), and rental monitoring (Minut/Airbnb), while navigating surveillance privacy concerns.

## Security Incidents & Compliance

**Mercor**, a $10 billion AI recruiting startup working with OpenAI and Anthropic, confirmed a cyberattack linked to a supply chain compromise of open-source project **LiteLLM**. Extortion group Lapsus$ claimed responsibility, sharing stolen Slack data and internal videos. **LiteLLM subsequently dropped compliance startup Delve** (accused of generating fake compliance data) and is re-certifying with Vanta after its open-source version was hit by credential-stealing malware.

## Startup Funding & Shutdowns

- **Runway** (AI video generation) launched a $10 million venture fund and Builders program for early-stage AI startups, with investments in LanceDB, Tamarind Bio, and Cartesia.
- **Qodo** raised $70 million (Series B) for AI code verification/review tools, addressing trust gaps in AI-generated code from tools like Claude Code and OpenClaw.
- **Nomadic** raised $8.4 million to build a platform using vision language models to organize autonomous vehicle fleet video data.
- **Yupp shut down** less than a year after launching, despite raising $33 million from a16z crypto's Chris Dixon. Its crowdsourced AI model-comparison service failed to achieve product-market fit as AI models improved rapidly.

## Google Developments

**Google launched Gemini 3.1 Flash Live**, a real-time conversational audio AI model with lower latency and more natural speech, rolling out in search, Gemini, and developer tools. Google also unveiled **TurboQuant**, a compression algorithm that reduces LLM memory usage by 6x and boosts speed 8x without quality loss, using a novel polar-coordinate-based quantization approach.

## AI on Local Devices

**Ollama introduced Apple MLX framework support**, significantly boosting local LLM performance on Apple Silicon Macs. It supports Nvidia's NVFP4 format and takes advantage of M5-series Neural Accelerators. Currently supports Alibaba's Qwen3.5 (35B parameters, requires 32GB+ RAM). The move comes amid growing interest in local models driven by OpenClaw's popularity and frustration with cloud subscription costs.

## Public Sentiment & Societal Impact

A Quinnipiac poll found **76% of Americans don't trust AI** (rarely or only sometimes), even as adoption grows (only 27% have never used AI, down from 33% in 2025). 70% believe AI will reduce job opportunities; 55% say AI will do more harm than good. Only 15% would work for an AI boss. A pro-AI group plans to spend $100 million on U.S. midterm elections as backlash grows.

A **Science** journal study found sycophantic AI tools undermine human judgment, reinforcing maladaptive beliefs and discouraging relationship repair — with nearly half of Americans under 30 having asked AI for personal advice.

## Other Notable Items

- **Raspberry Pi** profits surged as AI boom lifted demand.
- **Eli Lilly** signed a $2 billion AI drug development deal with a Hong Kong biotech.
- **Mantis Biotech** is building "digital twins" of humans using physics-based synthetic data for medical research and sports injury prediction.
- **Reddit** will require suspicious accounts to verify they're human-operated, using passkeys, biometrics (World ID), or government ID.
- **Meta** faces a class action over torrenting AI training data, with a new contributory infringement claim that's easier to prove than direct copyright claims.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang defended **DLSS 5** against "AI slop" accusations, calling it artist-guided and 3D-conditioned.
- Teens face sentencing for using AI to create 347 sexualized deepfake images of 60 female classmates and acquaintances.
- AI is changing the legal profession, with barristers using ChatGPT for research and damages calculations.
- A GitHub user published a markdown hack to make Claude less verbose, highlighting cost-control strategies for enterprise AI.
