## Summary

### Amazon/AWS & Anthropic Mega-Deal
Amazon is investing a fresh **$5 billion in Anthropic**, bringing its total commitment to $13 billion. In return, Anthropic has pledged to spend **over $100 billion on AWS over the next 10 years**, securing up to 5 GW of computing capacity to train and run Claude. The deal centers on Amazon's custom chips — **Graviton (CPU) and Trainium (AI accelerator)** — covering Trainium2 through Trainium4 (the latter not yet available). This mirrors a similar structure Amazon used when it joined OpenAI's $110 billion funding round two months earlier, contributing $50 billion. Anthropic may also be eyeing a new fundraising round at an $800 billion+ valuation.

### Anthropic — Government Relations & Mythos Model
Anthropic's **Mythos Preview**, a frontier cybersecurity model deemed too capable for public release, has been restricted to ~40 organizations. The **NSA is reportedly using Mythos** to scan for vulnerabilities, and the **UK's AI Security Institute** also has access — despite the Pentagon having labeled Anthropic a "supply-chain risk" after the company refused unrestricted military access to its models. Relations with the Trump administration appear to be **thawing**: CEO **Dario Amodei** met with White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent in what both sides called a "productive" meeting. Amodei has publicly stated he doesn't want AI "turned on our own people." The UK government warned that companies "should be worried" about Mythos's cybersecurity implications. Multiple FT analyses frame Mythos as testing the limits of global cyber defenses and signaling new "economics of AI scarcity."

### Anthropic — Products
Anthropic launched **Claude Design**, an experimental visual creation tool (prototypes, slides, one-pagers) powered by **Claude Opus 4.7**, available to Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers. It complements rather than competes with Canva, with export options including PPTX and direct Canva integration. The company also reported a **$50 billion deal with data center startup Fluidstack**.

### Cerebras Systems & AWS
AI chip startup **Cerebras Systems** filed for an IPO (planned for mid-May) after withdrawing a previous 2024 filing. The company raised a $1 billion Series H at a **$23 billion valuation** in February and reported $510 million in 2025 revenue. Notably, Cerebras struck an agreement with **AWS to deploy its chips in Amazon data centers** and landed a deal with **OpenAI reportedly worth over $10 billion**, taking fast-inference business from Nvidia.

### Google
Google expanded its **Gemini in Chrome** feature to multiple APAC markets (Australia, Indonesia, Japan, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Vietnam) on both desktop and iOS. The sidebar-based AI assistant integrates with Gmail, Google Photos, Calendar, and Maps, and uses **Nano Banana 2** for image transformation. The agentic browser-control feature remains US-only for paid AI Pro/Ultra subscribers.

### OpenAI
OpenAI is shedding "side quests" and refocusing on enterprise AI and its forthcoming "superapp." **Kevin Weil** (science research lead) and **Bill Peebles** (Sora creator) departed, along with CTO of enterprise applications **Srinivas Narayanan**. **Sora was shut down** after losing an estimated $1M/day in compute. OpenAI acquired personal finance startup **Hiro** and media company **TBPN** as small acqui-hires. The company is also working on an AI hardware device with Jony Ive.

### Bezos's Project Prometheus
Jeff Bezos's AI lab **Project Prometheus** is nearing a **$38 billion valuation** in a new funding deal, per the Financial Times.

### Fermi — AI Nuclear Power/Data Centers
AI nuclear power startup **Fermi** saw its CEO (Toby Neugebauer) and CFO (Miles Everson) suddenly depart, sending shares down **22%**. The company's nuclear-powered AI data center campus in Amarillo, Texas ("Project Matador") has faced friction with a key customer. The company attempted to frame the changes as "Fermi 2.0."

### Data Center Delays
The FT reports that **data centre construction delays threaten to choke AI expansion**, a constraint affecting cloud providers and AI infrastructure buildouts broadly.

### Cursor (AI Coding)
AI coding startup **Cursor** is raising **$2B+ at a $50 billion valuation**, led by Thrive and Andreessen Horowitz, with Nvidia and Battery Ventures also expected to participate. Revenue is forecast to exceed **$6 billion ARR** by end of 2026. The company recently achieved slight gross margin profitability through its proprietary Composer model.

### AI Coding Productivity ("Tokenmaxxing")
Despite massive token budgets becoming a status symbol, developer productivity analytics firm **Waydev** found that while AI-generated code acceptance rates appear to be 80-90%, real-world rates drop to **10-30%** after factoring in subsequent revisions — undercutting claims of increased productivity from tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex.

### AI & the Broader Economy
- **Northern Trust** says the AI boom is poised to be "massively disinflationary."
- Financial officials warn that latest AI models could **threaten the world banking system**.
- App Store launches are **up 60% YoY** in Q1 2026, with AI making app creation more accessible.
- AI-generated writing patterns (e.g., "It's not just X — it's Y") have **quadrupled** in corporate communications since 2023, appearing in filings from Cisco, Microsoft, McKinsey, and others.

### Other Notable Items
- **Uber** has committed over $10 billion to autonomous vehicle investments and purchases.
- **Tesla** expanded robotaxi service to Dallas and Houston (all three cities in Texas).
- **Sam Altman's World** (formerly Worldcoin) is integrating human verification into Tinder, ticketing, and email.
- **Recursive**, a months-old startup, raised $500 million for self-teaching AI.
- **China is probing Meta's $2 billion Manus deal**.
- **Palantir** published a 22-point manifesto denouncing inclusivity culture, amid scrutiny over its ICE work.
- **Chipmakers AMD, Arm, and Qualcomm** invested $60M in UK self-driving startup Wayve.
- Elad Gil advises AI startup founders to pre-schedule exit discussions, noting a **~12-month peak value window**.
