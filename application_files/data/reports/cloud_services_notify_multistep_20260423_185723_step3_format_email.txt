<!-- Cloud Services & AI Infrastructure -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:40px 48px 0;">
<p style="margin:0 0 4px;font-size:11px;font-weight:700;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6b7280;">Section</p>
<h2 style="margin:0 0 24px;font-size:22px;font-weight:700;color:#0f0f0f;letter-spacing:-0.3px;">Cloud Services & AI Infrastructure</h2>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>Google Cloud</strong> dominated this news cycle with announcements at <strong>Google Cloud Next</strong>. The company launched its 8th-generation custom AI chips, split into two variants — <strong>TPU 8t</strong> (training) and <strong>TPU 8i</strong> (inference) — claiming up to 3x faster training and 80% better performance per dollar, while still offering Nvidia's latest <strong>Vera Rubin</strong> chips. Google also unveiled the <strong>Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform</strong> (competing with Amazon's Bedrock AgentCore and Microsoft Foundry), which supports both Google's Gemini models and <strong>Anthropic's Claude</strong> (including the new Opus 4.7). Google introduced <strong>Workspace Intelligence</strong> with deep AI integration across Gmail, Sheets, Docs, and Chrome — including AI Overviews for enterprise Gmail, agentic "auto browse" capabilities in Chrome, and Gemini-powered spreadsheet automation.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>Thinking Machines Lab</strong> (Mira Murati's startup) signed a <strong>multibillion-dollar deal with Google Cloud</strong> for AI infrastructure, including access to Nvidia's GB300 chips for model training and deployment. This follows <strong>Anthropic</strong> signing agreements with both Google/Broadcom for TPU capacity and <strong>Amazon</strong> for up to 5 gigawatts of training/deployment capacity.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>Tesla</strong> raised its 2026 capital expenditure plan to <strong>$25 billion</strong> (up from $8.5B in 2025), largely for AI compute infrastructure, data centers, and robotics. Musk noted that <strong>Amazon</strong> has projected $200B in 2026 capex across AI, chips, robotics, and satellites, while <strong>Google</strong> plans $175-185B in capital spending.</p>

<h2 style="margin:0 0 24px;font-size:22px;font-weight:700;color:#0f0f0f;letter-spacing:-0.3px;">AI Models & Products</h2>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>OpenAI</strong> released <strong>GPT-5.5</strong>, described as its smartest model yet, advancing toward a unified "superapp" combining ChatGPT, Codex, and AI browser for enterprise customers. OpenAI also <strong>partnered with Infosys</strong> to integrate Codex into Infosys's Topaz AI platform, and launched <strong>Codex Labs</strong> with partners including Accenture, Capgemini, Cognizant, PwC, and TCS. OpenAI is also in talks to commit up to <strong>$1.5B to a private equity joint venture</strong>, while private equity firms are courting both OpenAI and Anthropic.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>SpaceX</strong> (merged with xAI) secured an option to <strong>acquire Cursor for $60 billion</strong>, preempting Cursor's planned $2B funding round at a $50B valuation. The deal, delayed until after SpaceX's summer IPO, includes a $10B payment regardless. This aims to bolster Musk's AI coding capabilities against Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>X</strong> (owned by xAI) launched <strong>Grok-powered Custom Timelines</strong> — AI-curated topic feeds available to Premium subscribers, where Grok reads and labels every post. X simultaneously shut down its Communities feature.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>Anthropic</strong> partnered with law firm <strong>Freshfields</strong> to create legal AI tools, while separately investigating <strong>unauthorized access to its powerful Mythos AI model</strong>. <strong>DeepSeek</strong> is targeting a <strong>$20B valuation</strong> to retain staff amid poaching pressure.</p>
