## Summary

### Cloud Infrastructure & Mega-Deals

**Amazon/AWS & Anthropic** struck a landmark deal: Amazon is investing a fresh **$5 billion** in Anthropic (bringing its total to $13B), and Anthropic committed to spending **over $100 billion on AWS over 10 years**, securing up to 5 GW of compute capacity. The deal centers on Amazon's custom **Trainium2 through Trainium4** chips, with options on future chip generations. Anthropic also separately signed an agreement with **Google and Broadcom** for multiple gigawatts of TPU capacity. VCs are reportedly offering Anthropic capital at an **$800B+ valuation**.

**Google Cloud** made major moves at **Cloud Next 2026** in Las Vegas. It earmarked a new **$750 million budget** to help partners (from startups to consulting firms) sell AI agents to enterprises—covering Gemini proof-of-concept projects, cloud credits, forward-deployed engineers, and deployment rebates. Google highlighted startups expanding on its cloud, including **Lovable** ($400M ARR track), **Notion** ($11B valuation, using Gemini), **Gamma** ($2.1B valuation, using Nano Banana 2 image model), and **Inferact** (vLLM creators, accessing Nvidia GPUs through Google Cloud).

**Google & Thinking Machines Lab** (Mira Murati's startup, formerly OpenAI CTO): A new **multi-billion-dollar deal** (single-digit billions) gives Thinking Machines access to Google Cloud AI infrastructure including systems with **Nvidia GB300 chips** for model training and deployment. This is the startup's first cloud provider deal (non-exclusive). Thinking Machines previously raised a **$2B seed at $12B valuation** and launched its first product, **Tinker**, which automates custom frontier AI model creation using reinforcement learning.

### AI Company Partnerships & Enterprise Expansion

**OpenAI partnered with Infosys** to integrate its tools (including coding assistant **Codex**) into Infosys's Topaz AI platform, targeting software engineering, legacy modernization, and DevOps. This is part of OpenAI's broader **Codex Labs** initiative, with partners including Accenture, Capgemini, CGI, Cognizant, PwC, and TCS. Codex now has **4M+ weekly active users**. Infosys's AI services generated ~$267M in its December quarter (~5.5% of revenue). OpenAI is also reportedly in talks to commit **up to $1.5 billion** to a private-equity joint venture.

**SpaceX struck a deal with Cursor** (the AI coding tool) to develop next-generation "coding and knowledge work AI," including a provision to **acquire Cursor for $60 billion** later this year—or pay $10B for its work. The partnership leverages SpaceX's Colossus supercomputer (equivalent of 1M Nvidia H100s). This follows xAI renting compute to Cursor and two senior Cursor engineers departing to join xAI under Elon Musk. Cursor's valuation has surged from $2.5B (Jan 2025) → $9B → $29.3B → potentially $50B+ in upcoming rounds.

### Google Cloud AI Product Launches

Google unveiled **generative AI features for Google Maps** and geospatial tools at Cloud Next: **Maps Imagery Grounding** (using Gemini to visualize projects in Street View), **Aerial and Satellite Insights** (analyzing satellite imagery via BigQuery), and two new **Earth AI Imagery models** for identifying objects like bridges, roads, and power lines. Partners include Airbus and Boston Children's Hospital.

Google expanded **Gemini in Chrome** to APAC countries (Australia, Indonesia, Japan, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Vietnam), adding sidebar-based AI assistant features, personal intelligence integration, and image transformation using Nano Banana 2. Agentic browser-control features remain US-only for paid plans.

### Anthropic's Mythos Security Concerns

Anthropic's powerful cybersecurity model **Mythos** faced an unauthorized access incident: a group accessed it through a **third-party vendor environment**, guessing the model's online location based on Anthropic's naming conventions. The group (from a Discord channel focused on unreleased AI models) demonstrated access to Bloomberg. Mythos was released only to select vendors (including **Apple**) under **Project Glasswing** due to concerns it could be weaponized. Anthropic's CEO also met with the **White House chief of staff** as the US government sought access to Mythos. Sam Altman called Anthropic's restricted release approach **"fear-based marketing"**, arguing it keeps AI in elite hands.

### Data Center & Infrastructure Turbulence

Shares in data center company **Fermi** plunged after top executives quit. Separately, **Jeff Bezos's Project Prometheus AI lab** is nearing a **$38 billion valuation** in a funding deal.

### Other Notable Developments

- **Meta** plans to record employee **keystrokes and mouse movements** on certain applications to train AI agents for computer-use tasks.
- **Apple CEO transition**: John Ternus succeeds Tim Cook, inheriting AI strategy challenges, antitrust battles, and privacy obligations.
- **NeoCognition** raised **$40M seed** to build self-learning AI agents that specialize like humans; backed by Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan and Databricks co-founder Ion Stoica.
- **OpenAI's ChatGPT Images 2.0** dramatically improved text rendering in AI-generated images.
- **YouTube** expanded AI **deepfake likeness detection** to celebrities and entertainment industry, with support from CAA, UTA, WME.
- **Clarifai** deleted 3M OkCupid photos used to train facial recognition AI, following an FTC settlement with Match Group.
- **Insurers** are moving to cap cyber payouts related to AI and "LLMjacking."
- **Sullivan & Cromwell** (elite law firm) admitted to AI hallucinations in its work.
- Northern Trust says the AI boom is poised to be **"massively disinflationary."**
- AI-generated writing tics (e.g., "It's not just X — it's Y") have **quadrupled** in corporate communications since 2023.
- **Recursive**, a months-old startup, raised **$500M** for self-teaching AI.
