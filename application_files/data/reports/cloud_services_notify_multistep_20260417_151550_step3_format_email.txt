<!-- AI Products & Platform Competition -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:36px 40px 0;">
<p style="margin:0 0 6px;font-size:11px;font-weight:600;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.2px;color:#6b7280;">Section 1</p>
<h2 style="margin:0 0 20px;font-size:21px;font-weight:700;color:#111111;letter-spacing:-0.3px;">AI Products &amp; Platform Competition</h2>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>Anthropic</strong> launched <strong>Claude Design</strong>, an experimental visual creation tool powered by <strong>Claude Opus 4.7</strong>, aimed at non-designers (founders, PMs) who need to quickly prototype slides, one-pagers, and app mockups. Outputs can be exported to Canva, PDF, PPTX, or URLs. The tool reads a company's codebase and design files to enforce brand consistency. This move prompted Anthropic CPO <strong>Mike Krieger</strong> to resign from <strong>Figma's</strong> board due to competitive conflicts — fueling "SaaSpocalypse" fears that major AI labs will displace established SaaS companies. Anthropic is reportedly turning away investors wanting to buy in at an <strong>$800 billion</strong> valuation. Separately, Anthropic is in talks to give the <strong>US government</strong> access to its <strong>Mythos</strong> model, and a UK minister warned that British companies "should be worried" about Anthropic's latest capabilities. CEO <strong>Dario Amodei</strong> stated he doesn't want AI "turned on our own people."
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>OpenAI</strong> revamped <strong>Codex</strong> with the ability to run background agents on a user's Mac — opening apps, clicking, and typing in parallel without interrupting the user's work — directly competing with Anthropic's Claude Code desktop control features. OpenAI also updated its <strong>Agents SDK</strong> with sandboxing, in-distribution harnesses for frontier models, enabling enterprises to build safer, long-horizon agents. Separately, OpenAI released a <strong>cybersecurity model</strong> to a limited customer group. Meanwhile, <strong>OpenAI investors are questioning its $852 billion valuation</strong> as its strategy shifts.
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>Google</strong> rolled out a side-by-side web browsing experience within <strong>AI Mode</strong> on Chrome, letting users click links that open alongside the AI conversation for follow-up questions. Google also introduced cross-tab search in AI Mode. On enforcement, Google blocked a record <strong>8.3 billion ads</strong> in 2025 using <strong>Gemini</strong> models, catching 99%+ of violating ads before display, though it suspended fewer advertiser accounts — signaling a shift toward blocking bad ads rather than banning bad actors.
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 4px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>Canva</strong> upgraded its AI assistant to act as an agentic design tool that calls various internal tools based on text prompts, creating layered, editable designs. COO Cliff Obrecht emphasized Canva works well with Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI as the "final mile" for editing and publishing.
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>Factory</strong>, an AI coding agent startup for enterprise engineering teams, raised <strong>$150M at a $1.5B valuation</strong> led by Khosla Ventures (with Sequoia, Insight, and Blackstone). Its differentiator is switching between foundation models (e.g., Claude, DeepSeek). Customers include Morgan Stanley, EY, and Palo Alto Networks. The broader AI coding race remains intense among Anthropic (Claude Code), Cursor, Cognition, and OpenAI (Codex).
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>Hightouch</strong> reached <strong>$100M ARR</strong>, with $70M added in the 20 months since launching AI-powered marketing content creation for brands like Domino's, Spotify, and PetSmart — enabling marketers to generate on-brand assets without design teams.
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 4px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>InsightFinder AI</strong> raised <strong>$15M Series B</strong> for AI agent observability — monitoring AI models, data, and infrastructure together to diagnose issues like model drift caused by infrastructure problems (e.g., outdated server cache).
</p>

Investors include Tiger Global and Premji Invest.
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 4px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong>Meta</strong> raised prices on <strong>Quest 3 and Quest 3S</strong> headsets ($50–$100 increases) due to the global <strong>RAM/memory chip shortage</strong>, joining Samsung, Microsoft, and Sony in passing component costs to consumers.
