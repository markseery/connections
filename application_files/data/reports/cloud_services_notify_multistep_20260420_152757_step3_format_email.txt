<h1>CoreWeave Dominates AI Cloud Infrastructure Headlines</h1>
<span class="ticker">NASDAQ: CRWV &nbsp;·&nbsp; April 2026</span>
</div>

<div class="content">
<p class="intro"><strong>CoreWeave (NASDAQ: CRWV)</strong> has had a blockbuster April 2026, securing a series of landmark deals that have sent its stock up ~50% and cemented its position as a leading AI cloud infrastructure provider.</p>

<div class="stat-grid">
<div class="stat-row">

</div>

</div>
</div>

<h2><span class="section-icon">🤝</span> Major Deals</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Meta Platforms</strong> — Expanded its AI cloud agreement to <strong>$21 billion</strong> (up from a prior $14.2B commitment), running through 2032, as Meta scales AI infrastructure beyond its own data centers.</li>
<li><strong>Jane Street</strong> — The trading firm signed a <strong>$6 billion multi-year AI cloud deal</strong> and made a separate <strong>$1 billion equity investment</strong> at $109/share, gaining access to Nvidia's upcoming Vera Rubin chips across CoreWeave data centers. Jane Street is now among CoreWeave's top five shareholders.</li>
<li><strong>Anthropic</strong> — A multi-year agreement to provide cloud computing for Anthropic's <strong>Claude AI models</strong>, making CoreWeave the infrastructure partner for 9 of the top 10 AI model providers (only xAI excluded).</li>
</ul>

<h2><span class="section-icon">💰</span> Financing &amp; Debt</h2>
<ul>
<li>CoreWeave raised <strong>$2.75 billion in senior notes</strong> (9.75% due 2031) and <strong>$3.5 billion in convertible notes</strong> (1.75% due 2032).</li>
<li>Together with <strong>Google</strong>, they raised a combined <strong>$6.7 billion in junk bonds</strong> — Google's portion was a record $5.7B offering led by Morgan Stanley, while CoreWeave contributed $1B. This underscores the massive AI infrastructure financing boom.</li>
<li>CoreWeave's heavy debt (~$21B long-term) is viewed by some analysts as a strategic growth indicator tied to contracted revenue, though others flag balance sheet risk.</li>
</ul>

<h2><span class="section-icon">🟢</span> Nvidia Connection</h2>
<div class="highlight-box">
<p><strong>Nvidia</strong> previously invested <strong>$2 billion</strong> in CoreWeave at $87.20/share, potentially growing its stake to ~11%. CoreWeave holds a <strong>$6.3B strategic collaboration</strong> with Nvidia and is named Nvidia's <strong>Exemplar Cloud for GB200 NVL72</strong> training workloads. Every major CoreWeave deal flows through Nvidia's GPU hardware stack.</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="section-icon">📊</span> Financial Profile</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Revenue backlog:</strong> ~$66.8–$88 billion; 2025 full-year revenue was $5.13B (+168% YoY); Q4 2025 revenue hit $1.6B.</li>
<li><strong>2026 guidance:</strong> $12–13 billion in revenue.</li>
<li><strong>Price target raised to $156</strong>; analysts see further upside. Jim Cramer called CRWV "a winner." Stock has risen ~172–190% since its March 2025 IPO.</li>
<li>CoreWeave announced its <strong>Q1 2026 earnings date</strong> and upcoming conference call.</li>
<li>Billionaire Philippe Laffont (Coatue) reportedly sold CoreWeave to buy another AI stock.</li>
</ul>

<hr class="divider">

<h2><span class="section-icon">☁️</span> Nebius</h2>
<p style="font-size:15px;color:#374151;margin:0 0 8px;"><strong>Nebius</strong>, a neocloud competitor, reported Q4 revenue of <strong>$228 million</strong> with strong YoY growth. Unlike CoreWeave's GPU-focused model, Nebius offers a full-stack AI development solution. Both companies are highlighted as high-growth AI infrastructure investments.</p>

<h2><span class="section-icon">🔍</span> Google</h2>
<p style="font-size:15px;color:#374151;margin:0 0 8px;"><strong>Google</strong> raised <strong>$5.7 billion</strong> in a record junk bond offering to fund AI data center expansion, part of the $6.7B combined deal with CoreWeave. Google remains both a CoreWeave customer and a major independent AI infrastructure builder.</p>

<h2><span class="section-icon">🏗️</span> Local Community Impact</h2>
<p style="font-size:15px;color:#374151;margin:0 0 8px;">CoreWeave's <strong>$1.8 billion AI data center</strong> under construction in <strong>Kenilworth, NJ</strong> (former Merck campus) faces resident pushback over water, flood risk, and electricity concerns. The mayor says CoreWeave will fund transmission upgrades, use closed-loop cooling, pay sales tax on power, and bring 140+ jobs. Over <strong>1,500 residents</strong> signed a petition opposing the project.</p>
