## Compute Products And Services

IREN offers three main service lines: **AI Cloud**, **Colocation**, and **Build-To-Suit** data centers.

**AI Cloud (IREN Cloud™):**
- Built to NVIDIA reference architecture with full non-blocking 3.2TB/s InfiniBand connectivity (NVIDIA Quantum-2 400Gb/s InfiniBand)
- GPU offerings listed on the AI Cloud page with specs:
  - **NVIDIA H100**: 80GB GPU memory, 2,048 GB RAM, 224 vCPUs, 30TB NVMe storage
  - **NVIDIA H200**: 141GB GPU memory, 2,048 GB RAM, 224 vCPUs, 30TB NVMe storage
  - **NVIDIA B200**: 180GB GPU memory, 2,048 GB RAM, 224 vCPUs, 15.4TB NVMe storage
  - **NVIDIA B300**: 288GB GPU memory, 3,096 GB RAM, 256 vCPUs, 30TB NVMe storage
  - **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72**: 288GB GPU memory, 20,736 GB RAM, 2,592 vCPUs, 276TB NVMe storage
- All pricing is "Contact sales" (no public pricing listed)
- No data ingress or egress fees
- Total fleet: approximately 22,200 NVIDIA GPUs spanning H100, H200, B200, B300, and GB300 NVL72 systems
- IREN expanded AI Cloud capacity to over 150,000 GPUs (per March 4, 2026 news release headline: "IREN Expands AI Cloud Capacity to 150,000 GPUs")
- Most recent expansion includes 7,100 NVIDIA HGX B300 GPUs and 4,200 NVIDIA HGX B200 GPUs
- Prince George facility: >23,000 GPUs installed or on-order
- IREN is a **Preferred NVIDIA Cloud Partner**
- Supports frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, JAX; Containers: Docker, Apptainer
- Storage: on-node storage and WEKA.IO storage across cluster
- NVIDIA NVLink interconnect for inter-GPU communication (up to 3.2 TB/s throughput)

**Data Center Infrastructure:**
- 6 locations across North America, >4.5 GW power secured
- 810 MW Operational, 2,100 MW Under Construction, 1,600 MW In Development
- 100% renewable energy (from clean/renewable sources or RECs)
- Locations and capacities:
  - **Sweetwater, TX**: 2,000 MW, >1,800 acres, under construction. Sweetwater 1: 1,400 MW (2026 substation energization), Sweetwater 2: 600 MW (2027). Capable of supporting over 700,000 liquid-cooled Blackwell GPUs. Air + liquid cooling.
  - **Childress, TX**: 750 MW, 576 acres, air + liquid cooling. Connected to ERCOT grid. Horizon 1 direct-to-chip liquid-cooled AI data center under construction. $9.7B Microsoft deal deploys 200MW of critical IT load in 4 phases through 2026.
  - **Oklahoma**: 1,600 MW, 2,000 acres, in development, 2028 substation energization, <6ms latency to network hubs
  - **Prince George, BC**: 50 MW, 12 acres, air-cooled, PUE avg. 1.1, max rack density 70 kW
  - **Mackenzie, BC**: 80 MW, 11 acres, air-cooled, PUE avg. 1.1, max rack density 70 kW
  - **Canal Flats, BC**: 30 MW, 10 acres, air-cooled, PUE avg. 1.1, max rack density 70 kW
- Build-To-Suit: 2.75GW of grid-connected power in West Texas, ~US$0.05/kWh all-in power cost (Source: Aurora Energy Research, Q4 2024)
- Sweetwater: <10ms latency to nearest hyperscaler regions

**Key Partnerships:**
- $9.7 billion multi-year agreement with Microsoft for GPU cloud infrastructure powered by NVIDIA GB300 GPUs (5-year contract, 20% prepayment, ~$1.94B annualized run-rate revenue)
- Dell Technologies: ~$5.8 billion GPU and equipment purchase agreement

**Colocation:**

**Differentiators cited:**
