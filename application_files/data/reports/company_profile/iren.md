## Products

**IREN Cloud™ (AI Cloud):**
GPU cloud service offering bare-metal NVIDIA GPU clusters for AI training and inference. Available GPU configurations:
- **NVIDIA H100**: 80GB GPU memory, 2,048 GB RAM, 224 vCPUs, 30TB NVMe storage
- **NVIDIA H200**: 141GB GPU memory, 2,048 GB RAM, 224 vCPUs, 30TB NVMe storage
- **NVIDIA B200**: 180GB GPU memory, 2,048 GB RAM, 224 vCPUs, 15.4TB NVMe storage
- **NVIDIA B300**: 288GB GPU memory, 3,096 GB RAM, 256 vCPUs, 30TB NVMe storage
- **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72**: 288GB GPU memory, 20,736 GB RAM, 2,592 vCPUs, 276TB NVMe storage

All clusters use NVIDIA Quantum-2 400Gb/s InfiniBand networking with 3.2TB/s bandwidth. No data ingress or egress fees. Supports frameworks including TensorFlow, PyTorch, JAX, and containers (Docker, Apptainer). Root access on bare metal servers. WEKA.IO storage available.

Fleet totals: approximately **22,200 NVIDIA GPUs** (including 7.1k HGX B300 and 4.2k HGX B200 GPUs); total expanded to over **150,000 GPUs** (as of March 2026); Prince George alone has **>23,000 GPUs** installed or on-order.

## Solutions

IREN frames three core solutions:

1. **AI Cloud** – Immediate access to NVIDIA GPU clusters for AI training and inference workloads. Designed for customers who need GPU compute without managing their own hardware. Use cases: fine-tuning LLMs (Llama, Mistral, Falcon, DeepSeek), deep learning, recommender systems, anomaly detection, digital twin simulations, multimodal AI, diffusion models.

2. **Colocation** – "Turnkey colocation data center solutions for your infrastructure needs." Rack-ready, AI-optimized space with air and liquid cooling, supporting NVIDIA Hopper and Blackwell GPUs. Includes 24/7 on-site personnel, multi-layered physical security, backup generators, battery backup systems. Available at four operational data center locations.

3. **Build-To-Suit** – "Custom-built data centers designed to your exact specifications." 2.75GW of grid-connected power across West Texas campuses. ~US$0.05/kWh all-in power cost (source: Aurora Energy Research, Q4 2024). Thousands of owned acres available. Master-planned sites with Tier 1 engineering partners. Modular expansion from 1 MW to 50+ MW. Positioned on major fiber paths.
