# Years in Business — Consolidated Summary

## Corporate Origins
- The company traces its roots back to **2000**, when founder **Arkady Volozh** began serving as CEO and Executive Director of the predecessor entity (Yandex).
- Long-standing board member **John Boynton** has been a Non-Executive Director since **2000** and Chairman since **2016**.

## Key Milestones
- **2011** — Initial public offering on **Nasdaq** (under the predecessor Yandex entity). Board member **Charles Ryan** joined as a non-executive director at this time.
- **November 2023** — The company was **publicly launched as Nebius**, marking its rebranding/spin-off from Yandex.
- **2024** — Arkady Volozh resumed the role of CEO (after stepping away in 2022).
- **October 2024** — The cloud platform was **fully relaunched**, having been "rewritten from scratch in less than a year."
- **July 2025** — Nebius celebrated its **first anniversary**, marked by a Nasdaq display in Times Square (aligning with the ~November 2023 public launch, roughly one year prior).

## Infrastructure
- The first data center in **Finland** was self-built (timeline not precisely dated but associated with the Nebius era).

## Summary Assessment
- **As a corporate lineage:** ~25 years in business (since 2000).
- **As the Nebius brand:** ~1.5–2 years since the public launch in November 2023.
- **As a publicly traded entity on Nasdaq:** ~14 years (since 2011 IPO).

## Products

# Nebius AI Cloud — Consolidated Product Summary

**Nebius AI Cloud** is a full-stack AI cloud platform purpose-built for building, training, and deploying AI models. It follows an "Aether" release series (versions 3.0, 3.1, 3.5). Key product components include:

## Compute
- **Compute Cloud** — Virtual machines with extensive NVIDIA GPU support (GB300 NVL72, GB200 NVL72, B300, B200, H200, H100, L40S, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell). Bare-metal performance with pre-installed GPU/network drivers.
- **GPU Clusters with InfiniBand** — NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand up to 3.2 Tbit/s per host; Quantum-X800 InfiniBand at 800 Gb/s.
- **Serverless AI (DevPods, Jobs, Endpoints)** — Container-based serverless compute for AI workloads.

## Orchestration & Cluster Management
- **Managed Service for Kubernetes®** — Container orchestration optimized for AI workloads.
- **Managed Soperator** — Fully managed Slurm-on-Kubernetes solution for AI training clusters.
- **Soperator** — Open-source Kubernetes operator for Slurm (available on GitHub).

## Storage
- **Object Storage** — S3-compatible with Standard and Enhanced classes. Enhanced offers up to 2 GB/s per GPU write throughput.
- **Shared Filesystem** — High-speed NVMe-based; up to 1 TB/s aggregate read throughput; scales linearly to 4 PB+.

## Data & ML Services
- **Managed Service for PostgreSQL®** — Fully managed database (GA).
- **Managed Service for MLflow** — ML lifecycle management (GA, MLflow 2.20.2).
- **Managed Service for Apache Spark™** — Data processing.

## Developer Tools
- **Container Registry** — Docker image storage and distribution.
