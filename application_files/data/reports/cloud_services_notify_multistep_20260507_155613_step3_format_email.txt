<td style="padding:16px 20px;background-color:#f9fafb;border-left:3px solid #0f0f0f;border-radius:0 8px 8px 0;margin-bottom:16px;">
<p style="margin:0 0 4px 0;font-size:15px;font-weight:700;color:#111111;">xAI becomes a cloud provider ("neocloud")</p>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:14px;line-height:1.65;color:#374151;">xAI and Anthropic announced a major partnership in which Anthropic is buying all compute capacity (~300MW) at xAI's Colossus 1 data center, likely worth billions. Musk explained xAI had migrated training to its newer Colossus 2 facility. This effectively turns xAI from a compute consumer into a cloud provider, monetizing its infrastructure and bolstering its balance sheet ahead of an expected IPO (combined with SpaceX, valued ~$1.25 trillion). The deal also lends credibility to SpaceX's orbital data center ambitions.</p>

<p style="margin:0;font-size:14px;line-height:1.65;color:#374151;">SpaceX is considering spending $55–119 billion on a vertically integrated semiconductor fabrication facility in Grimes County, Texas, partnering with Intel.
