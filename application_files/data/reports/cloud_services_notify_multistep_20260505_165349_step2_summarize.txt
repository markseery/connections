## Summary

### Cloud Services & Infrastructure

**India's Krutrim pivots to cloud:** India's first GenAI unicorn, Krutrim (founded by Ola's Bhavish Aggarwal), is abandoning AI model development in favor of **cloud services**, after pausing chip design efforts, pulling its AI assistant app, and cutting 200+ jobs. The startup generated ~$31.5M in revenue in FY2026 with its first annual profit, but has gone quiet on product updates as rivals like Sarvam advance.

**Cerebras IPO on track:** AI chipmaker **Cerebras Systems**, an OpenAI partner, is preparing a blockbuster IPO—selling 28M shares at $115–$125/share, potentially raising $3.5B at a ~$26.6B market cap. This would be the largest tech IPO of 2026. Investors include OpenAI's Sam Altman, Fidelity, Tiger Global, Coatue, AMD, and Abu Dhabi's G42.

**Peter Thiel backs ocean data centre startup** powered by wave energy, valued at $1B—reflecting growing interest in alternative infrastructure for AI compute.

**Banks seek to offload data centre debt risk**, signaling the massive capital requirements of AI infrastructure are straining traditional lenders.

### Enterprise AI & Joint Ventures

**Anthropic** launched a $1.5B enterprise AI joint venture backed by **Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, Hellman & Friedman**, plus Apollo, Sequoia, and others—deploying forward engineers into client workflows. Simultaneously, **OpenAI** is raising $4B for a parallel venture called "The Development Company" at a $10B valuation, with investors including TPG, Brookfield, Advent, and Bain Capital. Both ventures aim to capture enterprise AI deployment revenue through portfolio company access.

**Sierra** (led by Bret Taylor, also OpenAI's board chair) raised **$950M** at $15B+ valuation from Tiger Global and GV, claiming 40%+ of the Fortune 50 as customers and $150M ARR. Uber's CTO noted the company "blew through" its AI budget but now generates ~10% of code autonomously.

**OpenAI** president Greg Brockman defended the company's for-profit restructuring during the Musk trial, revealing a **$30B personal stake**. OpenAI recently raised $122B at an $852B valuation.

### AI National Security & Regulation

**Google, xAI (Musk), and Microsoft** agreed to **US national security reviews** of new AI models, a significant voluntary compliance step for frontier AI development.

The **Academy Awards** declared AI-generated actors and scripts ineligible for Oscars—performances must be human and consensual.

### Major AI Fundraising & Growth

**ElevenLabs** (voice AI) disclosed new Series D investors including **BlackRock, NVIDIA, Salesforce, Deutsche Telekom**, and celebrities. It surpassed **$500M ARR**, up from $350M at end of 2025, with enterprise contracts from Revolut and Klarna. Valuation reached $11B.

**CopilotKit** raised $27M (Series A) for its open-source AG-UI protocol enabling AI agents inside applications.

### AI Adoption Across Industries

**PayPal** CEO announced a push to become "cloud-native" and aggressively adopt AI in development, forming an "AI transformation" team—expecting $1.5B in cost savings alongside 20% workforce cuts over 2–3 years.

**Etsy** launched a native app within **ChatGPT** for natural-language product discovery, after its earlier Instant Checkout integration with OpenAI ended in March.

**Meta** is deploying AI to scan photos/videos for visual cues (height, bone structure) to identify underage users—rolling out in select countries.

**Google** AI tools are being trialed by English councils for planning decisions. **DoorDash** added AI tools for merchant onboarding, photo editing, and website generation.

**Image AI models** (from Google Gemini and ChatGPT) are driving 6.5x more app downloads than conversational model updates, per Appfigures data.

### AI & Jobs Debate

**Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang** argued AI is "creating an enormous number of jobs" and is America's best opportunity to re-industrialize, pushing back against displacement fears at the Milken Institute.

A **Harvard Medical School study** found OpenAI's o1 model outperformed two ER physicians in diagnostic accuracy, especially at initial triage.

### Musk vs. OpenAI Trial

The trial continued with Musk's expert witness Stuart Russell warning of AGI arms-race risks. OpenAI's lawyers revealed Musk texted Brockman a threatening settlement demand before trial ("you and Sam will be the most hated men in America"), which the judge ruled inadmissible.
