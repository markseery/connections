<!-- Section: Cloud Infrastructure & Data Centers -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:8px 40px 0 40px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 16px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#09090b;">Cloud Infrastructure &amp; Data Centers</h3>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding:0 40px 24px 40px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3f46;">
<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Anthropic</strong> significantly expanded its compute deal with <strong>Google Cloud</strong> and <strong>Broadcom</strong>, adding 3.5 gigawatts of TPU capacity (coming online in 2027) as part of its $50 billion U.S. compute infrastructure commitment. This follows skyrocketing demand for Claude AI models and Anthropic's recent $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion valuation.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Firmus</strong>, a Singapore-based data center company backed by <strong>Nvidia</strong>, raised $505 million at a $5.5 billion valuation (led by Coatue), bringing its six-month total to $1.35 billion. Firmus is building energy-efficient "AI factory" data centers in Australia using Nvidia's Vera Rubin next-gen platform (its "Project Southgate"), having pivoted from Bitcoin mining cooling.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Uber</strong> expanded its <strong>AWS</strong> contract to run more ride-sharing features on Amazon's Graviton chips and began trialing <strong>AWS's Trainium3</strong> AI chip—a notable shift given Uber's previous major cloud deals with <strong>Oracle</strong> and <strong>Google Cloud</strong> in 2023.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Iran</strong> threatened retaliatory strikes against U.S. tech infrastructure in the Middle East, specifically targeting the <strong>Stargate</strong> data center in the UAE (a $500B joint venture of <strong>OpenAI</strong>, <strong>SoftBank</strong>, and <strong>Oracle</strong>). Iranian missiles had already struck <strong>AWS</strong> data centers in Bahrain and an <strong>Oracle</strong> data center in Dubai.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>SpaceX</strong> is pursuing orbital data centers as part of its potential $1.75 trillion IPO narrative, while <strong>Intel</strong> joined Elon Musk's <strong>Terafab</strong> semiconductor project (alongside SpaceX and Tesla) to build a new U.S. chip fab in Texas, giving Intel's foundry business two major anchor customers.</p>

<p style="margin:0;">Insurers are turning to catastrophe bonds to offload growing data center risks. <strong>Poolside</strong>, an AI coding startup, is hunting for new data center partners after its deal with <strong>CoreWeave</strong> fell through.</p>
</td>

<tr>
<td style="padding:0 40px 24px 40px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#3f3f46;">
<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Anthropic</strong> previewed its powerful new frontier model <strong>Mythos</strong> through "Project Glasswing," a cybersecurity initiative with partners including <strong>Amazon</strong>, <strong>Apple</strong>, <strong>Broadcom</strong>, <strong>Cisco</strong>, <strong>CrowdStrike</strong>, <strong>Microsoft</strong>, the Linux Foundation, and Palo Alto Networks. The model reportedly found thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities in software. Anthropic faces tensions with the U.S. Defense Department, which labeled it a supply-chain risk, while the <strong>UK</strong> is courting Anthropic to expand in London.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Arcee</strong>, a 26-person startup, released its open-source 400B-parameter reasoning model "Trinity Large Thinking" on a $20M budget, positioning it as a Western alternative to Chinese open-source models, and gaining traction with OpenClaw users after Anthropic restricted Claude access for that tool.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Microsoft</strong> launched a "mid-class" AI model as compute limits bite, while its <strong>Copilot</strong> terms of use controversially state it is "for entertainment purposes only"—language Microsoft called "legacy" and promised to update.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>Google</strong> launched "AI Edge Eloquent," an offline-first Gemma-based dictation app, and added Gemini-powered photo captioning to Google Maps.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 14px 0;"><strong>OpenAI</strong> released ChatGPT app integrations (DoorDash, Spotify, Uber, etc.), published policy proposals for the AI economy (public wealth funds, robot taxes, four-day workweek), and acquired a popular tech talk show. Former OpenAI employees launched <strong>Zero Shot</strong>, a new ~$100M VC fund focused on AI.</p>

<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Samsung</strong> forecast record profits driven by the AI boom.</p>
