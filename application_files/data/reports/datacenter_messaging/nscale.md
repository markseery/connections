### Nscale Data Centers (Owned/Operated)

**Glomfjord, Norway**
- Located in northern Norway, just above the Arctic Circle; small industrial town of ~1,000 people
- **100% renewable energy** powered by hydropower dams (120MW hydro power plant built over 100 years ago)
- **30MW operational capacity, expandable to 60MW**
- "Perfectly suited to High Performance Computing (HPC) and the computational requirements of AI—it's cold, well connected, and there is a stable oversupply of renewable energy"
- Double redundant low-latency fibre connectivity
- Adiabatic cooling leveraging local Arctic climate
- Uses closed-loop water cooling system (cold water from fjord at 6-9°C, exits at ~34°C)
- ~85% of waste heat repurposed to nearby fish farms, exploring additional uses (swimming pools, road heating)
- No diesel generators — eliminated direct emissions from backup power
- PUE target of approximately 1.1
- Direct-to-chip liquid cooling for high-density GPU clusters
- Community investment: local infrastructure funding, job creation, waste heat reuse for community swimming pool
- Approved apprenticeship company in Nordland County; hosts students ages 16-18
- Featured in BBC StoryWorks' "Transforming Tomorrow" series
- Hosts Nscale's Svartisen Cluster which made the Top500 supercomputer list (AMD MI250X accelerators)
- Quotes: Alex Sharp: "I've helped build 54 data centers in my career but Glomfjord is different. It's powered 100% by renewable energy and built with purpose."
