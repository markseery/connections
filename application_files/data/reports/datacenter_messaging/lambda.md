### Datacenter Design & Architecture

- **Modular and interoperable**: "Lambda AI factories are modular and interoperable. New GPUs, interconnects, and cooling slots integrate as they ship, so capacity grows without disruptive retrofit."
- **Composable infrastructure model**: "Power, cooling, and physical space are designed as modular systems with clear interfaces, allowing each layer to scale and evolve independently."
- **Two deployment models**:
  - **Contiguous AI factories**: "75MW+ campuses for large clusters and mission-critical workloads"
  - **Distributed AI factories**: Regionally distributed architectures for compliance and performance needs
- **Rack density**: Ken Patchett: "Yesterday's data centers were between 2 and 15 kilowatts per rack, and now right next to those racks, we have to put a rack that takes 130 kilowatts or 240 kilowatts."
- **"Optimized rack power and cooling"** — combining direct-to-chip liquid and precision air cooling.
- Roadmap toward **"1 MW rack-scale designs."**
- **Comprehensive redundancy**: "All critical cooling systems are engineered with redundancy ranging from N+1 to N+N to ensure uninterrupted operation and maximum uptime."

---

### Cooling

- **Direct-to-chip (DTC) liquid cooling**: "Improves power usage effectiveness (PUE) by over 15% and enables high-density compute."
- **Precision air cooling**: "Handles CPU and storage loads, as well as any residual heat."
- **Hybrid cooling**: Combining liquid-to-the-chip and air cooling systems.
- Ken Patchett on cooling: "The chip proximity to one another is so dense that even the air flow between them isn't feasible."
- **100% direct-to-chip (D2C) liquid-cooled compute and switch trays** for NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and Vera Rubin NVL72 systems.
- NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 racks: **"4,000 pounds per system"** — "few data centers can handle the power density and cooling requirements."
