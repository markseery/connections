# Summary: CoreWeave Dominates AI Cloud Infrastructure Headlines

## Major Deals & Partnerships

**CoreWeave** is at the center of a massive AI infrastructure buildout, having secured several landmark agreements:

- **Meta** committed ~$21 billion in expanded AI cloud capacity through 2032, covering dedicated capacity across multiple sites and featuring early rollouts of **Nvidia's Vera Rubin** platform.
- **Anthropic** signed a multi-year deal for CoreWeave to power Claude AI models, with computing capacity scheduled to go live later in 2026. This deal is seen as a major validation of CoreWeave's neocloud model.
- **Jane Street** signed a $6 billion AI cloud agreement with CoreWeave.
- **OpenAI** has an existing five-year, $11.9 billion contract (including a $350 million private placement giving OpenAI a stake in CoreWeave). Wells Fargo estimates roughly a third of CoreWeave's contracted revenue is tied to OpenAI.

These deals could push CoreWeave's remaining performance obligations (backlog) past **$95 billion**, up from ~$66.8 billion at end of 2025.

## Financial Picture

CoreWeave posted **$5.13 billion in 2025 revenue** (up from $1.92B prior year) but recorded a **$1.17 billion net loss**. The company priced a **$1 billion offering of 9.750% senior notes due 2031** in April 2026 to fund continued data center expansion. Q1 2026 revenue is projected at ~$1.96 billion. Earnings are scheduled for **May 7** after market close.

## Executive Stock Sales & Insider Activity

Three senior CoreWeave executives sold **more than $1 billion** in shares in the first four months of 2026 — placing them in the **99th percentile** of S&P 500 insider selling. CEO Michael Intrator alone filed an April 28 sale valued at ~$32.8 million. **Magnetar Financial**, a 10% holder, unloaded ~1.28 million shares on May 1 for ~$154 million and sold call options on 1 million additional shares. The heavy selling has drawn scrutiny from compensation experts, given the company remains unprofitable.

## Analyst Sentiment & Stock Movement

- **Jefferies** raised its CoreWeave price target to **$160** (from $120), maintaining Buy, citing leadership in high-performance compute and a multi-trillion-dollar opportunity.
- **Citi** raised its target to **$155** (from $126), looking for 35–40% quarter-over-quarter backlog growth in Q1.
- **Goldman Sachs** reiterated Buy on **Apple** (noting App Store deceleration); other analyst calls covered **Amazon, Tesla, Palantir**.
- CoreWeave shares rose ~5.4–5.8% ahead of earnings, trading around $125, with a **21.87% short interest** adding volatility potential. The stock remains ~35% below its highs near $183.
- Jim Cramer called CoreWeave "aggressive" but with "terrific fundamentals," cautioning about volatility and the possibility of a financing-related surprise.

## Institutional Buying

New positions were disclosed by the **State of New Jersey Common Pension Fund D** and **Wealthfront Advisers** ($788K position), signaling growing institutional interest.

## Neoclouds vs. Google TPUs

**Nebius, Lambda, and CoreWeave** all stated they are **unlikely to adopt Google's TPUs** any time soon, preferring to stick with **Nvidia GPUs**. All three companies are Nvidia-backed, and they told The Information that Google's custom chips don't fit their current infrastructure strategies.

## CoreWeave Product Developments

CoreWeave launched **SUNK (SLURM on Kubernetes)** — a platform enabling AI researchers to run SLURM workloads on Kubernetes-based cloud infrastructure. It comes in two versions: SUNK Self-Service (templatized setup) and SUNK Anywhere (multi-cloud). The move targets the growing base of enterprise AI researchers beyond major labs.

## Broader AI Infrastructure Context

- Hyperscaler AI capex is now running **$700–725 billion** across companies like **Microsoft, Google, Amazon/AWS, Oracle**, and others.
- OpenAI's reported misses on user growth and revenue targets briefly rattled AI infrastructure stocks including CoreWeave and **Oracle**, though stocks recovered.
- The earnings week also features reports from **Arm Holdings, Palantir, Arista, and AppLovin**, with options markets pricing 10–14% post-earnings moves across AI-linked stocks.
