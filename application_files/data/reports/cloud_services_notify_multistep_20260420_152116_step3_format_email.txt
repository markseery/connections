<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:22px;font-weight:700;color:#111111;line-height:1.3;">OpenAI: Restructuring, Acquisitions &amp; Product Battles</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 14px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;">OpenAI is undergoing significant internal shifts. It acquired personal finance startup <strong>Hiro</strong> and new media company <strong>TBPN</strong> — small acqui-hires aimed at finding sticky consumer products beyond chatbots and improving its public image. Key executives departed: <strong>Kevin Weil</strong> (who led OpenAI for Science), <strong>Bill Peebles</strong> (creator of Sora, the AI video tool shut down after losing ~$1M/day in compute), and CTO of enterprise apps <strong>Srinivas Narayanan</strong>. The company is consolidating around enterprise AI and a forthcoming "superapp," shedding "side quests."</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 14px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;">OpenAI revamped <strong>Codex</strong>, its AI coding tool, adding background agentic capabilities on Mac desktops — a direct response to <strong>Anthropic's Claude Code</strong>, which is widely seen as winning the enterprise coding battle. OpenAI also released <strong>GPT-Rosalind</strong> for life sciences research.</p>

<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:22px;font-weight:700;color:#111111;line-height:1.3;">Anthropic: Mythos, Government Relations &amp; New Products</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 14px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;"><strong>Anthropic</strong> unveiled its <strong>Mythos</strong> model, described as too powerful for public release but demonstrated to Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent. <p style="margin:0 0 14px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#374151;">Anthropic also launched <strong>Claude Design</strong>, a visual prototyping tool powered by Claude Opus 4.7 for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise subscribers, with Canva export integration — targeting non-designers who need quick mockups.</p>
