# Summary: CoreWeave and the AI Cloud Infrastructure Boom

## CoreWeave's Massive Deal Pipeline & Financials

**CoreWeave (CRWV)** is at the center of the AI infrastructure trade, with a rapidly expanding roster of landmark agreements. The company announced a **$21 billion expanded AI infrastructure deal with Meta** (on top of a prior $14.2 billion arrangement, running 2027–2032), a **multi-year agreement with Anthropic** to power Claude AI models, and a **$6 billion AI cloud agreement with Jane Street**. CoreWeave also holds a **$11.9 billion five-year contract with OpenAI**, which included a $350 million private placement giving OpenAI a stake in the company. CEO Mike Intrator described the demand as insatiable, noting that even hyperscalers like Meta who build their own facilities also rely on CoreWeave.

For 2025, CoreWeave reported **$5.13 billion in revenue** (up from $1.92 billion the prior year) but a **net loss of $1.17 billion**. Its **revenue backlog stood at $66.8 billion**, having more than quadrupled during 2025. The company is targeting **$17–19 billion in ARR by 2026** and has a **$30 billion annual revenue objective** over time. It recently priced **$1 billion in 9.75% senior notes due 2031** and raised $3 billion in fresh debt.

## Stock Performance & Analyst Activity

CoreWeave stock surged ~6–8% on May 4, reaching ~$125–$130, with a market cap around $62–68 billion. **Citi raised its price target to $155 from $126** (Buy rating), projecting **35–40% quarter-over-quarter backlog growth** in Q1, driven by hyperscalers, AI labs, and enterprise clients. Other firms including **Oppenheimer and Wells Fargo** also maintained bullish ratings. The stock has high short interest (~21.87% of float, 64.65 million shares), adding volatility potential.

**Cathie Wood's ARK Invest** bought CoreWeave shares while trimming AMD and Bitcoin ETF exposure. **Institutional buyers** included Wealthfront ($788K position), Pictet Asset Management (62,328 shares), Mitsubishi UFJ Trust & Banking, and Universal Beteiligungs. Conversely, major shareholder **Magnetar Financial reduced its position by 21.91%**, selling millions of shares and call options for hundreds of millions of dollars in total proceeds.

## Q1 2026 Earnings on May 7

CoreWeave's first quarterly earnings report is scheduled for **May 7 after market close**. Analysts expect Q1 revenue near **$1.96 billion**. Key focus areas include backlog conversion rates, data center capacity expansion, and whether the company can translate its massive contracted pipeline into recurring revenue. Options markets are pricing **double-digit post-earnings moves** for CoreWeave and other AI-linked stocks reporting that week (including Arm, AppLovin, and Arista Networks).

## OpenAI Risk Factor

A significant overhang is **OpenAI concentration risk**. Wells Fargo estimates roughly **a third of CoreWeave's contracted revenue is tied to OpenAI**. Reports that OpenAI missed recent user and revenue targets rattled AI infrastructure stocks, with CoreWeave dropping 2.8% and **Oracle** also declining. OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar reportedly voiced internal concerns about meeting future compute costs if revenue didn't improve, creating a "ripple effect" across AI suppliers.

## Product & Platform Developments

CoreWeave launched **SUNK (SLURM on Kubernetes)** and **SUNK Anywhere**, making it easier for AI researchers to deploy SLURM cluster management workloads on Kubernetes-based cloud infrastructure, even beyond CoreWeave's own data centers. The company earned **SemiAnalysis' Platinum ClusterMAX rating twice** and set records in MLPerf benchmarking for AI training and inference. It also launched **CoreWeave ARENA** for validating AI workloads before production.

## Broader AI Infrastructure Context

**Hyperscaler AI capital expenditure has surged to $700–725 billion**, reinforcing GPU supply constraints and pricing strength across the sector. On May 4, **Nebius (NBIS) surged 12%** alongside CoreWeave's ~7% gain, while **Micron (MU)** led chip stocks. **IREN** and **Applied Digital (APLD)** were also noted among AI cloud movers. **Google** led AI stock performance heading into a busy earnings week, with **Palantir, Arista Networks, and CoreWeave** among those reporting. **Microsoft, Oracle, and the SoftBank-backed Stargate** were referenced as part of OpenAI's broader infrastructure lineup.

**CHAI AI**, backed by CoreWeave and AMD, reached **$80 million ARR** with a reported **$2.4 billion valuation**, underscoring the downstream ecosystem being built on CoreWeave infrastructure.

Jim Cramer was broadly bullish on CoreWeave, calling it "a winner" and discussing short-seller dynamics, while noting the coming earnings week would be "more prone to disappointment" than the mega-cap tech results that preceded it.
