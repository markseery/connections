<div class="deal-card">
<h3>🏦 Jane Street Deal — $7 Billion Total</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Jane Street</strong>, the quantitative trading firm, committed <strong>~$6 billion</strong> to use CoreWeave's AI cloud platform and made a separate <strong>$1 billion equity investment</strong> at <strong>$109 per share</strong>, making it one of CoreWeave's five largest shareholders.</li>
<li>The deal provides Jane Street access to <strong>NVIDIA's next-generation Vera Rubin</strong> chips across multiple CoreWeave data centers.</li>
<li>Jane Street described its needs in language akin to an AI research lab — training large, complex models on massive volumes of noisy financial data. CoreWeave's Max Hjelm called Jane Street "a frontier lab."</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="deal-card">
<h3>📘 Meta Deal — $21 Billion Expansion</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Meta</strong> expanded its commitment to CoreWeave to <strong>$21 billion</strong> (up from a prior $14.2 billion arrangement), running from 2027 to 2032. This brings Meta's total commitment to over <strong>$35 billion</strong>.</li>
<li>The deal includes capacity from data centers powered by <strong>NVIDIA Rubin</strong> chips.</li>
<li>Meta CEO-level commentary indicated they will continue building their own infrastructure while relying on CoreWeave to mitigate risk and meet insatiable AI compute demand.</li>
<li>CoreWeave also serves <strong>Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI</strong>.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="deal-card">
<h3>🤖 Anthropic Deal — Multi-Year, Undisclosed Value</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Anthropic</strong> signed a multi-year agreement with CoreWeave to power its <strong>Claude AI</strong> models, with a phased infrastructure rollout using various NVIDIA chip architectures.</li>
<li>With this addition, <strong>nine of the top ten AI model providers</strong> now use CoreWeave's platform (only <strong>xAI</strong> is not yet a customer).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<hr class="divider">

<p class="section-label">Financials</p>
<h2>Aggressive Capital Formation</h2>
<p>CoreWeave's fundraising has been equally aggressive:</p>
<ul>
<li>Raised <strong>~$3.5 billion in convertible senior notes</strong></li>
<li><strong>Upsized a high-yield bond offering</strong> due to heavy demand</li>
<li>Secured an <strong>$8.5 billion delayed draw term loan</strong> at <strong>investment-grade ratings</strong> — a first for a neocloud</li>
<li>CoreWeave now has a <strong>$66.8 billion revenue backlog</strong> and <strong>43 active data centers</strong>, though it posted a <strong>$1.17 billion net loss</strong> in 2025 while investing heavily in expansion</li>
</ul>
<p>Morningstar analyst Luke Yang noted that creditors are increasingly comfortable with neoclouds, with CoreWeave's latest facility being its first investment-grade debt at ~6% interest. Analysts see these take-or-pay deals from credible AI customers as de-risking the business model.</p>

<hr class="divider">

<p class="section-label">Industry Landscape</p>
<h2>Broader Cloud and AI Infrastructure Context</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tech giants plan to spend over $630 billion</strong> on capex in 2026, yet even that pace cannot meet demand for specialized GPU clusters, creating an opening for specialized providers like CoreWeave.</li>
<li><strong>Nebius</strong> is referenced as a competitor to CoreWeave in the neocloud space, with analysts comparing Nebius's accelerating growth against CoreWeave's massive contract scale.</li>
<li><strong>NVIDIA</strong> remains central to the entire ecosystem — CoreWeave's platform is built around NVIDIA GPUs (Blackwell, Hopper, and upcoming Vera Rubin architectures), and NVIDIA itself has <strong>$6.3 billion in capacity commitments</strong> with CoreWeave.</li>
<li><strong>OpenAI</strong> has contracts with CoreWeave totaling up to <strong>$22.4 billion</strong>.</li>
<li>CoreWeave's stock (CRWV) surged on the deals, with its RS rating jumping to 91 and its share price trading at a <strong>176% premium</strong> to its March 2025 IPO price. The stock was also upgraded by analysts and saw institutional buying from <strong>Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Group</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<hr class="divider">

<p class="section-label">Market Moves</p>
<h2>Investment Community Activity</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Billionaire Philippe Laffont</strong> (Coatue) sold CoreWeave and rotated into another AI stock.</li>
<li><strong>Zacks</strong> highlighted CoreWeave alongside <strong>Nebius, Bloom Energy, Oracle, Meta, and NVIDIA</strong> as key investment ideas in the AI infrastructure theme.</li>
<li><strong>Sandisk</strong> was announced as joining the Nasdaq-100 in the same week CoreWeave surged on its analyst upgrade.</li>
</ul>
