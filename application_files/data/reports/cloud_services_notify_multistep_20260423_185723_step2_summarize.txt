## Summary

### Cloud Services & AI Infrastructure

**Google Cloud** dominated this news cycle with announcements at **Google Cloud Next**. The company launched its 8th-generation custom AI chips, split into two variants — **TPU 8t** (training) and **TPU 8i** (inference) — claiming up to 3x faster training and 80% better performance per dollar, while still offering Nvidia's latest **Vera Rubin** chips. Google also unveiled the **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform** (competing with Amazon's Bedrock AgentCore and Microsoft Foundry), which supports both Google's Gemini models and **Anthropic's Claude** (including the new Opus 4.7). Google introduced **Workspace Intelligence** with deep AI integration across Gmail, Sheets, Docs, and Chrome — including AI Overviews for enterprise Gmail, agentic "auto browse" capabilities in Chrome, and Gemini-powered spreadsheet automation.

**Thinking Machines Lab** (Mira Murati's startup) signed a **multibillion-dollar deal with Google Cloud** for AI infrastructure, including access to Nvidia's GB300 chips for model training and deployment. This follows **Anthropic** signing agreements with both Google/Broadcom for TPU capacity and **Amazon** for up to 5 gigawatts of training/deployment capacity.

**Tesla** raised its 2026 capital expenditure plan to **$25 billion** (up from $8.5B in 2025), largely for AI compute infrastructure, data centers, and robotics. Musk noted that **Amazon** has projected $200B in 2026 capex across AI, chips, robotics, and satellites, while **Google** plans $175-185B in capital spending.

### AI Models & Products

**OpenAI** released **GPT-5.5**, described as its smartest model yet, advancing toward a unified "superapp" combining ChatGPT, Codex, and AI browser for enterprise customers. OpenAI also **partnered with Infosys** to integrate Codex into Infosys's Topaz AI platform, and launched **Codex Labs** with partners including Accenture, Capgemini, Cognizant, PwC, and TCS. OpenAI is also in talks to commit up to **$1.5B to a private equity joint venture**, while private equity firms are courting both OpenAI and Anthropic.

**SpaceX** (merged with xAI) secured an option to **acquire Cursor for $60 billion**, preempting Cursor's planned $2B funding round at a $50B valuation. The deal, delayed until after SpaceX's summer IPO, includes a $10B payment regardless. This aims to bolster Musk's AI coding capabilities against Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex.

**X** (owned by xAI) launched **Grok-powered Custom Timelines** — AI-curated topic feeds available to Premium subscribers, where Grok reads and labels every post. X simultaneously shut down its Communities feature.

**Anthropic** partnered with law firm **Freshfields** to create legal AI tools, while separately investigating **unauthorized access to its powerful Mythos AI model**. **DeepSeek** is targeting a **$20B valuation** to retain staff amid poaching pressure.

### GPU Demand & Compute Crunch

Astronomers are contributing to the global **GPU shortage** as NASA's upcoming Roman Space Telescope, the James Webb Space Telescope, and the Vera Rubin Observatory generate unprecedented data volumes. Researchers are transitioning galaxy-identification AI models from CNNs to transformer architectures, requiring significantly more compute. **Nvidia supplier SK Hynix** reported another record quarter, citing a "structural shift" in demand.

### Microsoft

**Microsoft** is offering **voluntary redundancy to 7% of staff** for the first time — a notable move as the company continues massive AI investments.

### Security & Compliance

Compliance startup **Delve's** troubles deepened: **Context AI**, a Delve customer, suffered a security breach that cascaded into a data breach at **Vercel** (hosted on Google). Context AI has since switched to **Vanta** for compliance. Previously, Delve customer **LiteLLM** was also compromised. **Y Combinator** severed ties with Delve. Separately, **insurers are moving to cap cyber payouts** related to AI and "LLMjacking" attacks. **Sullivan & Cromwell** admitted to AI "hallucinations" in legal work.

### Other Notable Items

- **Era** raised $11M for a software platform powering AI hardware devices
- **10x Science** raised $4.8M to accelerate drug characterization using AI and mass spectrometry
- **Beehiiv** expanded beyond newsletters with webinars, AI podcast analytics, and metered paywalls
- India's app market hit record revenue ($300M+ in Q1), with **Google One, ChatGPT, and YouTube** capturing most spending
- The **White House accused China** of "industrial-scale" theft of AI technology
- A Republican senator warned of **political costs for supporting AI**
- Reports examined the widening **AI digital divide** between high and low earners in the workplace
- **Eclipse Ventures** raised $1.3B for "physical AI" startups
