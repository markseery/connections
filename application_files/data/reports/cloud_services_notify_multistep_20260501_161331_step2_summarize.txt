# CoreWeave-Centric Cloud Infrastructure Summary

## Major Deals & Partnerships

**CoreWeave** has been on a massive deal-making spree, securing at least **$63.9 billion in contracts in Q1 2026 alone** (Wells Fargo estimates the true backlog exceeds $90 billion):

- **Meta**: Expanded AI infrastructure agreement worth **$21 billion** (on top of a prior $14.2 billion deal), running 2027–2032. Meta continues building its own facilities but relies on CoreWeave for additional GPU capacity, also serving **Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI**.
- **Anthropic**: Multi-year agreement to provide infrastructure for Claude AI models.
- **Jane Street**: Signed a **$6 billion** AI cloud agreement.
- **OpenAI & Perplexity**: Multi-year partnerships; CoreWeave is pursuing a **$3.1 billion leveraged loan** backed by OpenAI and Cohere contracts.
- **CHAI AI**: Backed by CoreWeave and AMD, CHAI AI has reached **$80M ARR** with a reported **$2.4B valuation**.

## Product & Platform Updates

- **SUNK Anywhere**: CoreWeave expanded its SUNK AI infrastructure platform to support **multi-cloud and on-premise** deployments via guided self-service, allowing AI research teams to standardize training clusters beyond CoreWeave's own data centers. This positions CoreWeave to compete more directly for enterprise AI workloads across hybrid environments.
- CoreWeave earned **SemiAnalysis' Platinum ClusterMAX™ rating** twice and continues to set records in **MLPerf benchmarking** for both AI training and inference.
- **Mission Control**: An operating system for AI at scale providing observability, security, and automated operations.

## Financial & Stock Performance

- **Stock (CRWV)**: Trading around $111–$114, up **~44% in 30 days**, **~152% over 1 year**, and **~41% YTD**. The stock recovered 61% from late-March lows.
- **Revenue**: ~$5.13 billion, with Q1 2026 revenues expected at $1.9–2.0 billion (up ~100% YoY). However, **earnings remain in loss** at ~$1.2 billion, with an expected Q1 loss of $0.90/share.
- **Q1 2026 earnings report** scheduled for **May 7**.
- **Debt financing**: Priced **$1 billion in 9.75% senior notes due 2031** and is eyeing an additional $3.1B leveraged loan.
- **Insider activity**: CEO Michael Intrator sold **200,000 shares (~$40.9 million)**; Vanguard Group purchased shares.
- **Analyst consensus**: Moderate Buy, average price target ~$125–128. Wells Fargo raised its target to $135 (Overweight); Morgan Stanley holds at $99; Mizuho holds at $105.
- **Cathie Wood (ARK Invest)** has been buying CRWV shares.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Nebius (NBIS)**: In talks to acquire Israeli AI firm **AI21 Labs** (after Nvidia exited negotiations). Nebius is also expanding data center capacity through contracts with CoreWeave and TD SYNNEX. Stock up 61% YTD. Nebius competes with **AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud** for AI infrastructure and applied AI workloads.
- **Microsoft (MSFT)**: DZ BANK maintained a Buy rating; Piper Sandler raised price target to $540. Analyst consensus is Strong Buy with an average target of ~$554.
- **Google Cloud**: Reported **63% growth**, outpacing Amazon and Microsoft cloud divisions.
- **Oracle**: Continues to back OpenAI alongside CoreWeave, though concerns persist about OpenAI missing some sales/user targets.
- **Iren** and **Nebius** stocks were noted as rising alongside CoreWeave on AI infrastructure momentum.

## Key Risks

- CoreWeave's earnings remain deeply negative with losses forecast to decline ~11.3%/year; high debt load and dilution history are flagged concerns.
- OpenAI reportedly missed some internal sales and user targets, which could affect Oracle and CoreWeave's infrastructure revenue tied to those contracts.
- Valuation debate: Some narratives suggest CoreWeave is significantly overvalued (fair value estimates as low as $70 vs. ~$112 trading price).
