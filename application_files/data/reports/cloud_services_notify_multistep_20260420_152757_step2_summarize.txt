# Summary: CoreWeave Dominates AI Cloud Infrastructure Headlines (April 2026)

**CoreWeave (NASDAQ: CRWV)** has had a blockbuster April 2026, securing a series of landmark deals that have sent its stock up ~50% and cemented its position as a leading AI cloud infrastructure provider.

## Major Deals

- **Meta Platforms**: Expanded its AI cloud agreement to **$21 billion** (up from a prior $14.2B commitment), running through 2032, as Meta scales AI infrastructure beyond its own data centers.
- **Jane Street**: The trading firm signed a **$6 billion multi-year AI cloud deal** and made a separate **$1 billion equity investment** at $109/share, gaining access to Nvidia's upcoming Vera Rubin chips across CoreWeave data centers. Jane Street is now among CoreWeave's top five shareholders.
- **Anthropic**: A multi-year agreement to provide cloud computing for Anthropic's **Claude AI models**, making CoreWeave the infrastructure partner for 9 of the top 10 AI model providers (only xAI excluded).

## Financing & Debt

- CoreWeave raised **$2.75 billion in senior notes** (9.75% due 2031) and **$3.5 billion in convertible notes** (1.75% due 2032).
- Together with **Google**, they raised a combined **$6.7 billion in junk bonds** — Google's portion was a record $5.7B offering led by Morgan Stanley, while CoreWeave contributed $1B. This underscores the massive AI infrastructure financing boom.
- CoreWeave's heavy debt (~$21B long-term) is viewed by some analysts as a strategic growth indicator tied to contracted revenue, though others flag balance sheet risk.

## Nvidia Connection

- **Nvidia** previously invested **$2 billion** in CoreWeave at $87.20/share, potentially growing its stake to ~11%. CoreWeave holds a $6.3B strategic collaboration with Nvidia and is named Nvidia's Exemplar Cloud for GB200 NVL72 training workloads. Every major CoreWeave deal flows through Nvidia's GPU hardware stack.

## Financial Profile

- **Revenue backlog**: ~$66.8–$88 billion; 2025 full-year revenue was $5.13B (+168% YoY); Q4 2025 revenue hit $1.6B.
- **2026 guidance**: $12–13 billion in revenue.
- **Price target raised to $156**; analysts see further upside. Jim Cramer called CRWV "a winner." Stock has risen ~172–190% since its March 2025 IPO.
- CoreWeave announced its **Q1 2026 earnings date** and upcoming conference call.
- Billionaire Philippe Laffont (Coatue) reportedly sold CoreWeave to buy another AI stock.

## Nebius

- **Nebius**, a neocloud competitor, reported Q4 revenue of **$228 million** with strong YoY growth. Unlike CoreWeave's GPU-focused model, Nebius offers a full-stack AI development solution. Both companies are highlighted as high-growth AI infrastructure investments.

## Google

- **Google** raised **$5.7 billion** in a record junk bond offering to fund AI data center expansion, part of the $6.7B combined deal with CoreWeave. Google remains both a CoreWeave customer and a major independent AI infrastructure builder.

## Local Community Impact

- CoreWeave's **$1.8 billion AI data center** under construction in **Kenilworth, NJ** (former Merck campus) faces resident pushback over water, flood risk, and electricity concerns. The mayor says CoreWeave will fund transmission upgrades, use closed-loop cooling, pay sales tax on power, and bring 140+ jobs. Over 1,500 residents signed a petition opposing the project.

## Kirkland & Ellis

- The law firm advised CoreWeave on its $2.75 billion notes offerings.
