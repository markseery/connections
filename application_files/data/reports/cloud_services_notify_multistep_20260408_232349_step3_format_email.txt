<!-- Cloud & Infrastructure -->
<tr>
<td style="padding:32px 48px 8px;">
<h2 style="margin:0;font-size:13px;font-weight:700;color:#888888;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;">Cloud &amp; Infrastructure</h2>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding:16px 48px 32px;">
<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">AWS/Amazon</strong> is navigating dual investments in competing AI labs. AWS CEO Matt Garman defended Amazon's $50 billion investment in OpenAI alongside its existing $8 billion commitment to Anthropic, arguing that AWS has long managed partner-competitor relationships. Separately, <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Uber expanded its AWS contract</strong>, adopting Amazon's Graviton CPUs and trialing Trainium3 AI chips—a notable shift given Uber's prior multi-year cloud commitments to <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Oracle and Google Cloud</strong>. Jeff Bezos's <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Project Prometheus</strong> also poached an xAI co-founder from OpenAI, signaling Amazon's broader AI ambitions.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Anthropic</strong> signed an expanded compute deal with <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Google Cloud and Broadcom</strong> for increased TPU capacity (reportedly 3.5 gigawatts per a Broadcom SEC filing), coming online in 2027. This extends Anthropic's $50 billion U.S. compute infrastructure commitment. Anthropic also previewed its new frontier model <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Mythos</strong> through "Project Glasswing," a cybersecurity initiative with partners including <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike</strong>, and others. The model identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities. Anthropic recently closed a $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion valuation. The <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">UK is actively courting Anthropic</strong> to expand in London following its clash with the U.S. Defense Department, which labeled it a supply-chain risk.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Google</strong> quietly launched "AI Edge Eloquent," an offline-first dictation app on iOS using Gemma-based models, and added Gemini-powered AI photo captioning to Google Maps.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Microsoft</strong> launched a "mid-class" AI model as compute limits bite, reflecting growing pressure to offer efficient alternatives amid constrained GPU supply.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Meta</strong> released its first AI model since Zuckerberg's massive spending spree.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Firmus</strong>, a Singapore-based AI data center provider backed by <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Nvidia</strong>, raised $505 million at a $5.5 billion valuation (led by Coatue), bringing total fundraising to $1.35 billion in six months. Its "Project Southgate" is building energy-efficient AI data centers in Australia using Nvidia's next-gen <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Vera Rubin</strong> platform. Originally a Bitcoin mining cooling company, Firmus is the latest crypto-to-AI pivot.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Intel</strong> joined Elon Musk's <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Terafab</strong> semiconductor project alongside SpaceX and Tesla, providing the chip fabrication expertise those companies lack. The factory aims to produce AI and robotics chips in Texas.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Samsung</strong> forecast record profits driven by the AI boom.</p>

<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;">Insurers are turning to <strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">catastrophe bonds</strong> to offload growing data center risks, reflecting the sector's rapid physical expansion.</p>
</td>

<tr>
<td style="padding:16px 48px 32px;">
<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Arcee</strong>, a 26-person startup, released its open-source 400B-parameter reasoning model "Trinity Large Thinking" on a $20 million budget, positioning it as a Western alternative to capable Chinese models and a popular choice for OpenClaw users after Anthropic restricted Claude access.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">OpenAI</strong> released a child safety blueprint developed with NCMEC, acquired a popular tech talk show, and continues expanding its ChatGPT app ecosystem—<strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Tubi</strong> became the first streaming service to launch a native app within ChatGPT.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 18px;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Databricks</strong> co-founder Matei Zaharia won the ACM Prize in Computing, declaring "AGI is here already" while the company hits $5.4 billion revenue run rate at a $134 billion valuation.</p>

<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:25px;color:#333333;"><strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Atlassian</strong> launched visual AI tools (Remix) and third-party agents in Confluence using MCPs, connecting to Lovable, Replit, and Gamma.</p>
