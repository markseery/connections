# Summary: CoreWeave and the AI Cloud Infrastructure Boom

## CoreWeave's Massive Deal Momentum

**CoreWeave** (NASDAQ: CRWV) has emerged as the dominant "neocloud" AI infrastructure provider, securing a wave of landmark deals that have driven its stock up ~50-64% and pushed its pro forma revenue backlog past **$88 billion**.

**Key deals:**
- **Meta**: Expanded its commitment by $21 billion (on top of a prior $14.2B arrangement), totaling ~$35B running through 2027–2032. Meta, which guided $115–135B in 2026 capex, needs CoreWeave's GPU capacity to supplement its own data centers.
- **Jane Street**: Signed a **$6 billion** AI cloud agreement and purchased **$1 billion** in CoreWeave equity at $109/share, gaining access to Nvidia's upcoming Vera Rubin chips across CoreWeave data centers.
- **Anthropic**: Entered a multi-year agreement for CoreWeave to power its Claude AI models. CoreWeave now serves 9 of the top 10 AI model providers (xAI being the exception).
- **Nvidia**: Previously invested $2 billion, potentially growing its stake to ~11%, endorsing CoreWeave's position in GPU-powered cloud services.

## Financing and Capital Markets

CoreWeave has been aggressively raising capital:
- **$3.5 billion** in 1.75% convertible notes due 2032 (~30% premium, signaling strong institutional demand)
- **$1.75 billion** in 9.75% senior notes due 2031, followed by an additional **$1 billion** add-on at 102% of par
- **Google** and CoreWeave together raised **$6.7 billion** in junk bonds — Google accounted for $5.7B (led by Morgan Stanley) and CoreWeave $1B

CoreWeave's founders gained **$4 billion** in personal wealth in one week during the stock surge. The stock trades near $117, up 172% since its 2025 IPO, at roughly 12.2x sales vs. a 1.8x US IT average.

## Financial Profile and Valuation Debate

CoreWeave reported **$5.1B revenue** in 2025 (up 168% YoY) with a **$1.2B net loss**. Wall Street projects ~142% revenue growth in 2026 and 87% in 2027, with estimates of $23.3B revenue by end of 2027. Analysts are split: bulls see the stock trading at ~5x 2026 sales with massive contracted backlog; bears see a capital-intensive leasing model with heavy leverage and hardware obsolescence risk. One user narrative prices fair value at $70 (overvalued), while others see upside to $133+.

Billionaire **Philippe Laffont** (Coatue) sold CoreWeave and rotated into Nvidia. **Jim Cramer** called CoreWeave a winner. Institutional investors including **KBC Group**, **Farther Finance Advisors**, and **Bosman Wealth Management** have taken new positions.

## Google (Alphabet)

**Google** raised **$5.7 billion** in high-yield bonds as part of the AI infrastructure financing wave, alongside CoreWeave's $1B issuance. Google is also a CoreWeave customer, reflecting the trend of hyperscalers supplementing their own data centers with neocloud capacity.

## Nebius

**Nebius** is a fellow neocloud provider offering a full-stack AI development solution (vs. CoreWeave's GPU-focused model). Nebius reported **$228M in Q4 revenue** with analysts projecting **522% revenue growth** in 2026 and 195% the following year — the fastest growth among peers. Wall Street expects $9.7B in Nebius revenue by end of 2027. Meta is spending with both CoreWeave and Nebius as part of its **$48 billion** AI infrastructure commitment.

## Microsoft, Nvidia, and Other Hyperscalers

- **Microsoft** is among CoreWeave's customers, alongside Google and OpenAI.
- **Nvidia** is the critical hardware backbone — every dollar spent on CoreWeave flows through Nvidia GPUs. Nvidia holds a $6.3B strategic collaboration with CoreWeave and named it an "Exemplar Cloud."
- **Meta** is guiding $115–135B in 2026 capex, driving compute demand across multiple cloud providers.
- **Amazon** was separately noted as investing up to another $25 billion in Anthropic as part of an AI infrastructure deal.

## Local Community Impact

CoreWeave's **$1.8 billion data center** under construction in **Kenilworth, NJ** (on the former Merck campus) is facing resident pushback over water usage, flood risk, and electricity costs. The mayor defended the project, noting CoreWeave uses a closed-loop cooling system, will fund transmission upgrades, and will bring 100+ jobs and significant tax revenue.

## Applied Digital

**Applied Digital** was also mentioned as a high-growth AI infrastructure company alongside CoreWeave and Nebius.
