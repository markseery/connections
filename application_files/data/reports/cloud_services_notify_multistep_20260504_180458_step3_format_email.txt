<tr>
<td style="padding:40px 40px 0 40px;">
<h2 style="margin:0 0 20px 0; font-size:20px; font-weight:700; color:#0d0d0d; border-left:4px solid #7b8aff; padding-left:14px;">CoreWeave's Massive Deal Pipeline &amp; Financials</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
<strong>CoreWeave (CRWV)</strong> is at the center of the AI infrastructure trade, with a rapidly expanding roster of landmark agreements. The company announced a <strong>$21 billion expanded AI infrastructure deal with Meta</strong> (on top of a prior $14.2 billion arrangement, running 2027–2032), a <strong>multi-year agreement with Anthropic</strong> to power Claude AI models, and a <strong>$6 billion AI cloud agreement with Jane Street</strong>. CoreWeave also holds an <strong>$11.9 billion five-year contract with OpenAI</strong>, which included a $350 million private placement giving OpenAI a stake in the company. CEO Mike Intrator described the demand as insatiable, noting that even hyperscalers like Meta who build their own facilities also rely on CoreWeave.
</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
For 2025, CoreWeave reported <strong>$5.13 billion in revenue</strong> (up from $1.92 billion the prior year) but a <strong>net loss of $1.17 billion</strong>. Its <strong>revenue backlog stood at $66.8 billion</strong>, having more than quadrupled during 2025. The company is targeting <strong>$17–19 billion in ARR by 2026</strong> and has a <strong>$30 billion annual revenue objective</strong> over time. It recently priced <strong>$1 billion in 9.75% senior notes due 2031</strong> and raised $3 billion in fresh debt.
</p>
</td>
</tr>

<h2 style="margin:0 0 20px 0; font-size:20px; font-weight:700; color:#0d0d0d; border-left:4px solid #7b8aff; padding-left:14px;">Stock Performance &amp; Analyst Activity</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
CoreWeave stock surged ~6–8% on May 4, reaching ~$125–$130, with a market cap around $62–68 billion. <strong>Citi raised its price target to $155 from $126</strong> (Buy rating), projecting <strong>35–40% quarter-over-quarter backlog growth</strong> in Q1, driven by hyperscalers, AI labs, and enterprise clients. Other firms including <strong>Oppenheimer and Wells Fargo</strong> also maintained bullish ratings. The stock has high short interest (~21.87% of float, 64.65 million shares), adding volatility potential.
</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
<strong>Cathie Wood's ARK Invest</strong> bought CoreWeave shares while trimming AMD and Bitcoin ETF exposure. <strong>Institutional buyers</strong> included Wealthfront ($788K position), Pictet Asset Management (62,328 shares), Mitsubishi UFJ Trust &amp; Banking, and Universal Beteiligungs. Conversely, major shareholder <strong>Magnetar Financial reduced its position by 21.91%</strong>, selling millions of shares and call options for hundreds of millions of dollars in total proceeds.

<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
CoreWeave launched <strong>SUNK (SLURM on Kubernetes)</strong> and <strong>SUNK Anywhere</strong>, making it easier for AI researchers to deploy SLURM cluster management workloads on Kubernetes-based cloud infrastructure, even beyond CoreWeave's own data centers. The company earned <strong>SemiAnalysis' Platinum ClusterMAX rating twice</strong> and set records in MLPerf benchmarking for AI training and inference. It also launched <strong>CoreWeave ARENA</strong> for validating AI workloads before production.

<h2 style="margin:0 0 20px 0; font-size:20px; font-weight:700; color:#0d0d0d; border-left:4px solid #7b8aff; padding-left:14px;">Broader AI Infrastructure Context</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
<strong>Hyperscaler AI capital expenditure has surged to $700–725 billion</strong>, reinforcing GPU supply constraints and pricing strength across the sector. On May 4, <strong>Nebius (NBIS) surged 12%</strong> alongside CoreWeave's ~7% gain, while <strong>Micron (MU)</strong> led chip stocks. <strong>IREN</strong> and <strong>Applied Digital (APLD)</strong> were also noted among AI cloud movers. <strong>Google</strong> led AI stock performance heading into a busy earnings week, with <strong>Palantir, Arista Networks, and CoreWeave</strong> among those reporting. <strong>Microsoft, Oracle, and the SoftBank-backed Stargate</strong> were referenced as part of OpenAI's broader infrastructure lineup.
</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
<strong>CHAI AI</strong>, backed by CoreWeave and AMD, reached <strong>$80 million ARR</strong> with a reported <strong>$2.4 billion valuation</strong>, underscoring the downstream ecosystem being built on CoreWeave infrastructure.
</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 0 0; font-size:15px; line-height:1.7; color:#333333;">
Jim Cramer was broadly bullish on CoreWeave, calling it "a winner" and discussing short-seller dynamics, while noting the coming earnings week would be "more prone to disappointment" than the mega-cap tech results that preceded it.
