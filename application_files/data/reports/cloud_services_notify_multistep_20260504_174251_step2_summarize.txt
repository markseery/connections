## Summary

### Cloud Services & Infrastructure

**Pentagon AI Contracts:** The U.S. Department of Defense signed deals with **Nvidia, Microsoft, Amazon Web Services (AWS)**, and **Reflection AI** to deploy AI technology and models on classified networks (IL6/IL7) for military use. This follows earlier agreements with **Google, SpaceX, and OpenAI**. The move accelerates after the Pentagon's dispute with **Anthropic** over usage guardrails — Anthropic won an injunction against being labeled a "supply-chain risk." Over 1.3 million DOD personnel now use the Pentagon's GenAI.mil platform.

**Big Tech Earnings — Cloud as Winner:** AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure all posted strong numbers, signaling that enterprise AI spending is materializing primarily through cloud infrastructure. Cloud was called "the winner of earnings week."

**Peter Thiel-Backed Ocean Data Centre:** Peter Thiel backed a **$1 billion ocean data centre startup** powered by wave energy, representing a novel approach to AI infrastructure.

**Banks and Data Centre Debt:** Banks are seeking to offload risk to avoid "choking" on the massive volume of data centre debt being generated by the AI buildout.

---

### AI Lab Fundraising & Valuations

**Anthropic** is closing a ~$50 billion fundraise at a valuation expected to exceed **$900 billion** — potentially surpassing OpenAI's $852 billion valuation. The round may close within two weeks. Anthropic's annual revenue run rate is reportedly near $40 billion. It simultaneously announced a **$1.5 billion joint venture** with **Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs** (plus Apollo, General Atlantic, Sequoia, and others) for enterprise AI deployment.

**OpenAI** is raising $4 billion for a parallel enterprise JV called **"The Development Company"** at a $10 billion valuation, backed by TPG, Brookfield, Advent, and Bain Capital. Both ventures follow the forward-deployed engineer model (à la Palantir) to embed AI in enterprise customers. OpenAI previously closed $122 billion at an $852 billion valuation.

**Sierra** (led by Bret Taylor, also OpenAI's chairman) raised **$950 million** led by Tiger Global and GV at a $15B+ valuation. It claims 40%+ of the Fortune 50 as customers and $150M ARR, positioning itself as the "global standard" for AI-powered customer experiences. Uber's CTO noted the company "blew through" its AI budget after deploying agentic tools, with 10% of code now generated autonomously.

---

### Musk vs. OpenAI Trial

Elon Musk's lawsuit to unwind OpenAI's for-profit conversion continued in court. His sole AI expert witness, Berkeley professor **Stuart Russell**, testified about AGI risks and the arms-race dynamics among frontier labs. OpenAI's lawyers revealed Musk texted Greg Brockman and Sam Altman before trial, threatening "you will be the most hated men in America" if they didn't settle — the judge ruled the texts inadmissible. Musk seeks to strip **Microsoft's** licensing agreement, require OpenAI's tech to be public, and obtain damages.

---

### Cybersecurity Tools

**OpenAI** restricted access to its new **GPT-5.5 Cyber** tool to verified cybersecurity defenders — mirroring the same approach by **Anthropic** with its **Mythos** tool that Sam Altman had previously criticized. OpenAI's Trusted Access for Cyber (TAC) program has scaled to thousands of verified defenders.

---

### Other Cloud/AI-Adjacent Developments

- **Apple** saw unexpected AI-driven demand for Macs; Mac mini and Mac Studio sold out as users ran local AI models. Mac revenue hit $8.4B, up 6% YoY. Startups are challenging Apple over App Store curbs on AI "vibe coding" apps.
- **Huawei's** chip sales are projected to jump 60% as Chinese tech groups shift away from **Nvidia**.
- **Meta** acquired humanoid robotics startup **Assured Robot Intelligence (ARI)** to join its Superintelligence Labs, advancing its humanoid AI and potential AGI research.
- **Replit** is tracking toward $1B ARR with up to 300% net revenue retention; rival **Cursor** is reportedly in talks for a $60B acquisition by SpaceX.
- **Google** AI tool is being trialed by English councils to speed up planning decisions.
- **DoorDash** launched AI tools using approaches similar to **Amazon's** 2024 merchant onboarding tool.
- **Legora** (legal AI) hit $5.6B valuation with **Nvidia's NVentures** investment, competing with Harvey.
- A Harvard study found **OpenAI's o1 model** outperformed human ER doctors in diagnostic accuracy.
- The **Academy Awards** declared AI-generated performances and scripts ineligible for Oscars.
