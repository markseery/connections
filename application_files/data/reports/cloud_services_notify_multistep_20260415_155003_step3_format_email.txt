<!-- Section: Cloud Services -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 24px 0;font-size:13px;font-weight:700;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6366f1;border-bottom:2px solid #6366f1;padding-bottom:8px;">Cloud Services &amp; GPU-as-a-Service Providers</h2>

<table role="presentation" width="100%" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">
<tr>
<td style="padding-bottom:28px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 8px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;">Allbirds pivots to AI cloud computing</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">After selling its shoe brand for $39 million, Allbirds is rebranding as <strong>NewBird AI</strong>, a "GPU-as-a-Service and AI-native cloud solutions provider." The company secured $50 million in convertible financing and plans to acquire GPU assets to offer AI compute capacity to customers, with potential future M&amp;A activity. The move leverages its existing Nasdaq-listed public shell (ticker: BIRD). Both TechCrunch and the Financial Times covered this story.</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding-bottom:28px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 8px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;">Parasail raises $32M for inference cloud</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;"><strong>Parasail</strong>, a cloud computing startup focused on AI inference, raised a $32 million Series A. The company generates 500 billion tokens daily, renting processing time across 40 data centers in 15 countries rather than owning all its own hardware. Founded by former <strong>Groq</strong> executive Mike Henry, Parasail orchestrates workloads across rented GPUs and liquidity markets to drive down inference costs, betting on the proliferation of open-source models and growing demand from developers wanting cheap, fast tokens ("tokenmaxxing").</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding-bottom:36px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 8px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;">Kepler Communications — orbital compute</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">Canada's <strong>Kepler Communications</strong> has the largest compute cluster in orbit (~40 Nvidia Orin edge processors on 10 satellites linked by laser). It now has 18 customers, including <strong>Sophia Space</strong>, which will test passively cooled space computer software on Kepler's constellation. Large-scale orbital data centers (envisioned by <strong>SpaceX</strong> and <strong>Blue Origin</strong>) aren't expected until the 2030s.</p>
</td>
</tr>
</table>

<!-- Section: Anthropic vs OpenAI -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 24px 0;font-size:13px;font-weight:700;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6366f1;border-bottom:2px solid #6366f1;padding-bottom:8px;">Major AI Lab Competition: Anthropic vs. OpenAI</h2>

<table role="presentation" width="100%" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">
<tr>
<td style="padding-bottom:28px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 8px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;">Anthropic's rapid rise is pressuring OpenAI</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">Anthropic's annualized revenue surged from $9 billion (end of 2025) to $30 billion (end of March 2026), driven largely by coding tools. Its $380 billion valuation is increasingly seen as a relative bargain compared to <strong>OpenAI's</strong> $852 billion valuation, which some of OpenAI's own investors now view skeptically. Secondary-market demand for Anthropic shares has grown "nearly insatiable" while OpenAI shares trade at a discount. OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar pointed to its record $122 billion fundraise as evidence of confidence, but one investor compared OpenAI to "the Netscape of AI."</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding-bottom:28px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 8px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;">Anthropic's Mythos model</h3>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#334155;">Deemed too dangerous to release publicly due to powerful cybersecurity capabilities, Anthropic's Mythos model is generating major waves. Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark confirmed the company briefed the <strong>Trump administration</strong> on Mythos. Trump officials (Treasury Secretary Bessent and Fed Chair Powell) encouraged major banks — <strong>JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley</strong> — to test Mythos for vulnerability detection. <strong>UK financial regulators</strong> are also rushing to assess its risks. Cybersecurity stocks fell on concerns Anthropic's tool could disrupt the sector. This is all happening while Anthropic is simultaneously suing the Trump DOD over being labeled a supply-chain risk after disputes over military use of its AI.</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding-bottom:36px;">
<h3 style="margin:0 0 8px 0;font-size:18px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;">OpenAI updates</h3>
