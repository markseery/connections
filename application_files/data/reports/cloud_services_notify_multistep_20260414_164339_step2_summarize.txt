## Summary

### Cloud Services & Infrastructure

**Microsoft** is developing an OpenClaw-like AI agent to integrate into its **Microsoft 365 Copilot** for enterprise customers, adding to its growing roster of agentic tools including Copilot Cowork (which runs in the cloud and is powered partly by **Anthropic's Claude**) and Copilot Tasks. The new agent would offer better security controls than the open-source OpenClaw. Microsoft has also tapped Anthropic's Claude as an option alongside its own models.

**Google** expanded its Gemini "Personal Intelligence" feature to India, allowing AI Pro and AI Ultra users to connect Gmail, Google Photos, and YouTube for personalized answers. Google plans to extend it to free users soon. Google also launched Gemini in Chrome and enabled agentic restaurant booking via AI mode in India through partners like Zomato and Swiggy. Separately, the **FT** examined whether Google's **TurboQuant** algorithm could reduce AI demand for memory chips.

**Kepler Communications** (Canada) launched the **largest orbital compute cluster** — roughly 40 Nvidia Orin edge processors across 10 satellites linked by laser communications — and now has 18 customers. Partner **Sophia Space** will test passively cooled space-computer software on Kepler's constellation. Large-scale orbital data centers (envisioned by **SpaceX** and **Blue Origin**) aren't expected until the 2030s. **AWS** speaker Rob Chu is featured at the SusHi Tech Tokyo conference alongside Nvidia, Google, and Microsoft.

The **AI infrastructure build-out is powering global goods trade**, according to the FT.

### Anthropic / Claude Rising

**Anthropic's Claude** emerged as the dominant AI chatbot at the HumanX conference in San Francisco, with vendors and panelists consistently preferring it over ChatGPT. Anthropic is **closing in on OpenAI** in U.S. business usage. Its new **Mythos model** — noted for exceptional vulnerability-detection capabilities — prompted **Treasury Secretary Bessent and Fed Chair Powell** to summon bank CEOs (JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Bank of America, Morgan Stanley) to encourage testing. **UK financial regulators** are also rushing to assess Mythos's risks, and **cybersecurity stocks fell** on fears the model could disrupt the sector. Anthropic is simultaneously fighting the Trump administration in court over a DoD supply-chain risk designation.

Anthropic **temporarily banned OpenClaw creator Peter Steinberger** (now at OpenAI) from accessing Claude, though the ban was quickly reversed. Anthropic had previously changed pricing so OpenClaw usage requires separate API billing — a so-called "claw tax" — since agentic compute patterns exceed normal subscription usage.

### OpenAI Under Pressure

**OpenAI** faces mounting challenges: investors are **questioning its $852 billion valuation** as strategy shifts. The company acquired **Hiro Finance** (an AI personal finance startup) in what appears to be an acqui-hire. It launched a new **$100/month Pro plan** for ChatGPT, explicitly targeting Anthropic's pricing, with 5× more Codex coding capacity than the $20 Plus plan. The $200 tier remains available.

CEO **Sam Altman** responded to both a **Molotov cocktail attack on his home** and a critical **New Yorker profile** questioning his trustworthiness. **Florida's attorney general** announced an investigation into OpenAI over ChatGPT's alleged role in a deadly FSU shooting. A **stalking victim sued OpenAI**, claiming ChatGPT fueled her abuser's delusions and the company ignored three warnings. OpenAI is also backing an Illinois bill that would shield AI labs from liability.

### Apple & App Store Friction

**Apple** removed vibe-coding app **Anything** twice from the App Store, citing rules against downloading/executing code, part of a broader crackdown affecting **Replit** and Vibecode. Anything is pivoting to desktop. Separately, Apple is testing **four designs for smart glasses** planned for 2027 — no displays, but with camera, calls, music, and Siri integration, competing with **Meta's Ray-Ban** glasses.

### Other Notable Developments

- **Vercel** CEO signaled IPO readiness as AI agents fuel ARR growth to $340M (from $100M in early 2024).
- **Meta** built an AI version of Mark Zuckerberg to interact with staff; it also paused contracts with **Mercor** ($10B-valued AI data startup) after a major data breach via compromised open-source tool LiteLLM.
- **Alibaba** is shifting from open-source AI toward revenue-generating models.
- **China** is luring top AI talent back from Silicon Valley.
- **Stanford's annual AI report** highlights a growing disconnect between AI insiders (focused on AGI) and the public (worried about jobs, utility costs, and safety).
- A study found **AI chatbots misdiagnose in over 80%** of early medical cases.
- **Palantir** received a Trump endorsement after a short-seller attack.
- **Carson Block** sees AI disruption as a new opportunity for short sellers.
- AI-generated client queries risk **pushing up lawyers' fees**.
