</td>
</tr>

</td>

<tr>
<td style="padding:20px 40px 0;">
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>OpenAI closed a record $122 billion funding round</strong> at an $852 billion valuation, co-led by SoftBank, Andreessen Horowitz, D.E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, and T. Rowe Price, with participation from <strong>Amazon, Nvidia, and Microsoft</strong>. About $3 billion came from retail investors, and the company will be included in ARK Invest ETFs ahead of an expected IPO. OpenAI claims $2 billion/month in revenue, 900 million weekly active users, 50 million subscribers, and an ads pilot generating over $100 million ARR in under six weeks. The company expanded its revolving credit facility to $4.7 billion.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>OpenAI shut down Sora</strong>, its AI video-generation tool, just six months after launch. User count had collapsed to under 500,000 while burning ~$1 million/day in compute costs. The decision was driven by a strategic refocus on enterprise/developer tools ahead of a possible IPO — partly in response to <strong>Anthropic's Claude Code</strong> gaining momentum. <strong>Disney's planned $1 billion partnership</strong> tied to Sora collapsed; Disney was reportedly blindsided, learning of the shutdown less than an hour before the public announcement. No money had changed hands.</p>

<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>OpenAI added plugin support to Codex</strong>, its agentic coding tool, enabling integrations with GitHub, Gmail, Cloudflare, Vercel, and others — catching up to similar features already available in Claude Code. OpenAI also <strong>indefinitely shelved plans for an erotic ChatGPT "adult mode"</strong> after backlash from investors, staff, and advisors who warned it could create a "sexy suicide coach."</p>
</td>
</tr>

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td style="padding:20px 40px 0;">
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>Anthropic accidentally leaked the full source code of Claude Code</strong> — nearly 2,000 TypeScript files and 512,000+ lines of code — when version 2.1.88 of its npm package included a source map file. Security researcher Chaofan Shou discovered it immediately. Anthropic called it "a release packaging issue caused by human error, not a security breach." This came just days after Anthropic accidentally made ~3,000 internal files publicly available, including details of an unannounced model. The leak revealed Claude Code as "a production-grade developer experience, not just a wrapper around an API."</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>Claude Code gained new computer-control capabilities</strong>, allowing it to point, click, and navigate users' screens to complete tasks — available to Pro and Max subscribers on MacOS as a "research preview." Anthropic is also <strong>battling the Department of Defense</strong> over AI use, with the Trump administration blocked from punishing Anthropic over the Pentagon dispute. <strong>Google</strong> is reportedly nearing a deal to help finance a multibillion-dollar data center leased to Anthropic.</p>

<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">Anthropic published a report on AI's labor market impact, claiming LLMs could theoretically perform 80%+ of tasks across many job categories, though the underlying methodology relied on speculative 2023-era assumptions.</p>

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td style="padding:20px 40px 0;">
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>Mistral AI raised $830 million in debt</strong> to build an Nvidia-powered data center near Paris (operational Q2 2026) and announced $1.4 billion in Swedish AI infrastructure investment, aiming to deploy 200MW of compute across Europe by 2027. CEO Arthur Mensch emphasized reducing dependence on third-party cloud providers.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>Starcloud raised $170 million (Series A)</strong> at a $1.1 billion valuation to build data centers in space, backed by Benchmark and EQT Ventures. It launched its first satellite with an <strong>Nvidia H100 GPU</strong> in November 2025 and plans a version with <strong>Nvidia Blackwell</strong> chips and an <strong>AWS server blade</strong>. Cost competitiveness depends on SpaceX's Starship achieving frequent commercial launches (potentially 2028-2029).</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>ScaleOps raised $130 million</strong> at an $800 million valuation for software that automatically manages and reallocates computing resources (GPUs, memory, storage) in real time, claiming up to 80% reduction in cloud and AI infrastructure costs. The founder previously worked at <strong>Run:ai</strong> (acquired by <strong>Nvidia</strong>).</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>AI chip startup Rebellions</strong> (South Korea) raised $400 million in a pre-IPO round at a $2.34 billion valuation, bringing total funding to $850 million. It launched RebelRack and RebelPOD inference infrastructure platforms and is expanding to the U.S., Japan, Saudi Arabia, and Taiwan, targeting <strong>cloud providers, neoclouds</strong>, government agencies, and telecoms.</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>Nvidia invested $2 billion in chipmaker Marvell</strong> to boost AI networking. UK chip startup <strong>Fractile</strong> is seeking to raise $200 million to challenge Nvidia.</p>

<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;"><strong>U.S. Senators Warren and Hawley</strong> urged the Energy Information Administration to mandate annual electricity-use disclosures from data centers, amid growing consumer concerns about energy costs. 65% of Americans oppose building AI data centers in their communities. The <strong>FT</strong> asked whether the AI data center boom could become a "$9 trillion bust," and a top energy developer warned about overbuilding power supplies for AI.</p>
