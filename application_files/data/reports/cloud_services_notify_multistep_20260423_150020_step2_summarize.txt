## Summary: CoreWeave and the AI Cloud Infrastructure Buildout

**CoreWeave (CRWV)** is at the epicenter of the accelerating AI infrastructure buildout, rapidly signing massive contracts and raising capital to fund GPU-centric cloud capacity.

### Major Customer Deals
- **Meta** expanded its commitment to CoreWeave by $21 billion (running 2027–2032), on top of a prior $14.2 billion arrangement, totaling ~$35 billion. The infrastructure will span multiple locations including NVIDIA Vera Rubin deployments.
- **Anthropic** signed a multi-year agreement with CoreWeave to power its Claude AI models, sending the stock up ~11%. CoreWeave now serves 9 of the top 10 AI model providers (only xAI excluded).
- **Jane Street** signed a $6 billion AI cloud agreement with CoreWeave.
- CoreWeave's total backlog stands at **$66.8 billion** with pre-sold capacity.

### Cross-Cloud & Google Cloud Partnership
CoreWeave launched **"CoreWeave Interconnect,"** a fiber-based direct link to **Google Cloud** enabling cross-cloud AI training and inference. It leverages Google Cloud's Partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect. Additionally, CoreWeave introduced **SUNK Anywhere** (supports multi-cloud training across Google Cloud, AWS, Azure, and on-premises) and **LOTA Cross-Cloud** (cross-cloud data storage/workload portability). This aligns with the broader **AWS–Google Cloud multicloud interoperability initiative**, which Microsoft Azure plans to join later in 2026.

### Debt & Capital Raises
- CoreWeave completed a **$1.0 billion add-on offering of 9.750% Senior Notes due 2031** at 102%, bringing total outstanding notes to $2.75 billion. Total debt stands at ~$21.6 billion.
- **Core Scientific (CORZ)** is raising **$3.3 billion via junk bonds** to fund six AI data centers leased to CoreWeave under a 12-year agreement potentially generating ~$10 billion in revenue. Core Scientific pivoted from bitcoin mining to AI hosting. A separate firm, **Edged Compute**, is marketing $1.3 billion in bonds for facilities leased to CoreWeave and an Alibaba unit.

### Nebius
- **Meta** also committed up to **$27 billion in AI capacity with Nebius**, another neocloud provider. Motley Fool articles compared CoreWeave vs. Nebius as investment options, with Meta spending a combined ~$48 billion across both neoclouds.

### Stock & Insider Activity
- CoreWeave trades at ~$122/share (~10x trailing sales, ~$62 billion market cap), rated **Hold** by Seeking Alpha due to execution risk, capital intensity, and heavy interest costs despite locked-in demand.
- Stock surged ~30% in one week driven by the Anthropic and Meta deals; founders' stakes rose by ~$4 billion.
- Significant **insider selling**: Brian Venturo entities sold 1.125 million shares (~$110–118/share); Magnetar-affiliated funds sold ~643,000+ shares; Brannin McBee sold 200,000 shares.
- **Asset Management One** made a new $4.8 million investment in CRWV.
- Jim Cramer highlighted CoreWeave as a winner but warned investors to expect volatility.

### Broader AI Infrastructure Trends
- AI infrastructure borrowers have raised **$17.9 billion in junk bonds** in 2026 alone, underscoring the capital-intensive nature of the buildout.
- Neoclouds like CoreWeave and Nebius are emerging as a **critical layer** alongside hyperscalers (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure), with hyperscalers increasingly relying on external GPU-centric platforms to scale faster.
- CoreWeave's Q1 2026 earnings call is scheduled for **May 7, 2026**.
