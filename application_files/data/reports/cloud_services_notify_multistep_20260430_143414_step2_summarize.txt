## Summary

### Big Tech Cloud & AI Earnings (Q1 2026)

**AWS (Amazon)** posted a standout quarter with net sales of $37.6 billion, up 28% YoY — its fastest growth in 15 quarters. CEO Andy Jassy attributed the surge to AI demand, noting AWS's AI revenue run rate now exceeds $15 billion. Amazon warned that capital expenditure will keep rising as it invests in land, power, buildings, chips, and networking. AWS also immediately began offering **OpenAI** products (models, Codex, and a new "Bedrock Managed Agents" service) after Microsoft lost its exclusivity over OpenAI's technology.

**Google Cloud** surpassed $20 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, a 63% YoY increase. AI solutions were the primary growth driver, with genAI-based products growing ~800% YoY and API token usage hitting 16 billion per minute. However, Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai acknowledged growth was **capacity-constrained** — cloud backlog doubled to $462 billion — and said revenue would have been higher if demand could be met. Google is **outpacing rivals** in planned AI spending, with Big Tech's combined AI capex plans rising to an estimated **$725 billion**.

**Microsoft** reported its AI business exceeded a **$37 billion annual run rate**, up 123% YoY. CEO Satya Nadella said the revised OpenAI deal — which ends Microsoft's exclusivity but gives it royalty-free access to OpenAI's frontier models through 2032 — is still favorable. He plans to "fully exploit" that IP access. **Microsoft 365 Copilot** now has over **20 million paid enterprise seats**, with weekly engagement matching Outlook usage levels. Copilot queries per user rose ~20% QoQ, and major deals include Accenture (740,000+ seats). Nadella emphasized Copilot is multi-model, also supporting Anthropic's Claude.

**Meta** posted $56.3 billion in Q1 revenue (+33% YoY) and $26.8 billion net income (+61%). Its business AI tools hit **10 million conversations/week** (up from 1 million in early 2026), powered by the new Muse Spark LLM from its Meta Superintelligence Labs. Over 8 million advertisers use its genAI ad creative tools. Meta raised its 2026 capex forecast to **$125–$145 billion**, largely due to higher memory component costs. Reality Labs continued losing ~$4 billion/quarter ($83.5 billion cumulative since 2021). China blocked Meta's **$2 billion acquisition of AI group Manus**.

### OpenAI / Microsoft / Amazon Realignment

OpenAI and Microsoft formally **loosened their exclusive partnership**. Microsoft retains royalty-free access to OpenAI's most advanced AI through 2032 but no longer has exclusivity. OpenAI immediately expanded its **Amazon/AWS relationship**, making models, Codex, and AI agent tools available on AWS Bedrock. Microsoft, meanwhile, is deepening ties with **Anthropic** for agent products powered by Claude.

### Anthropic's Massive Fundraise

**Anthropic** has received multiple preemptive offers to raise **~$40–50 billion** at an **$850–900 billion valuation**, potentially its last private round before an IPO. Its annual revenue run rate has surged past $30 billion (up from ~$9 billion at end of 2025), driven heavily by AI coding products (Claude Code, Cowork). A board decision on the round is expected in May. Goldman Sachs separately stopped bankers from using Claude in Hong Kong.

### SoftBank's Data Center Robotics Venture

**SoftBank** is creating **Roze AI**, a new company that would deploy autonomous robots to build data centers more efficiently in the U.S. The conglomerate is already eyeing a **$100 billion IPO** potentially in H2 2026, though some insiders are skeptical about the valuation and timeline.

### Infrastructure & Data Center Themes

The FT explored the **"great American data centre divide,"** while Big Tech collectively planned **$725 billion in AI infrastructure spending**. Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon are all aggressively expanding capacity, with Google explicitly noting it is compute-constrained. Memory chipmakers hope AI demand will end their traditional boom-and-bust cycles.

### Other Notable Items

- **Parallel Web Systems** (founded by ex-Twitter CEO Parag Agrawal) raised a $100M Series B at a **$2 billion valuation** for its AI agent web-search APIs.
- **Runway** ($5.3B valuation) is expanding beyond AI video into general world models for gaming, robotics, and real-time media generation.
- **Firestorm Labs** raised $82M for containerized drone manufacturing platforms deployable near front lines.
- **Scout AI** raised $100M to train military AI models ("Fury") for autonomous logistics and weapons.
- **Elon Musk** testified in his OpenAI lawsuit, claiming Altman "stole a charity" and admitting under oath that Tesla is not currently pursuing AGI — contradicting his own recent tweet.
- **Google** told staff it is "proud" of a Pentagon AI contract following internal employee backlash; separately, Google employees urged CEO Pichai to block U.S. military AI use.
- Google launched new Gemini features across Google TV, Google Photos (AI digital closet), and gained 25M new paid subscriptions in Q1.
- Amazon launched AI-powered audio Q&A ("Join the chat") on product pages.
