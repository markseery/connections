# Summary: Cloud Services Provider News (Late March – Early April 2026)

## CoreWeave — Dominant Headlines

**Financing & Debt:** CoreWeave closed an **$8.5 billion Delayed Draw Term Loan (DDTL 4.0)** backed by GPUs and a long-term Meta contract. The new facility carries a significantly lower interest rate (SOFR + 2.25% vs. 4.00% previously) and shifts from asset-based borrowing limits to an execution-based, project-style model incorporating power availability. The stock jumped ~12% on the news. However, CoreWeave's total debt now exceeds **$21 billion** (up from ~$8B in 2024), with quarterly interest expenses of $388 million consuming nearly a third of revenue.

**Revenue & Growth:** CoreWeave reported **110% YoY revenue growth** in Q4 2025, with full-year 2025 revenue hitting **$5.1 billion** (up 168%). Its contractual backlog surged 300%+ to **$66.8 billion**. Management forecasts 2026 revenue of $12–13 billion. Despite top-line growth, the company operated at a loss for Q4 and the full year, with plans to spend up to **$35 billion in 2026** on infrastructure buildout. Analysts expect profitability within five years.

**Poolside Deal Collapse:** CoreWeave and AI startup **Poolside** mutually terminated their partnership on **Project Horizon**, a planned 2 GW data center campus in West Texas. CoreWeave's new $8.5B loan gave it flexibility to shift away from fixed single-tenant commitments toward multi-partner leasing. Poolside is now seeking alternative partners, including reportedly **Google**, and also lost a planned Nvidia-backed $2 billion funding round.

**Applied Digital Lease Deal:** CoreWeave amended an existing data center lease with **Applied Digital** (APLD), suspending terms on two of four data halls and entering a new lease covering those halls through a CoreWeave subsidiary, sending Applied Digital stock higher.

**Customer Wins:** CoreWeave was selected by **Zonos** to power cross-border commerce solutions and by **Cline** to power autonomous engineering workloads. The company is now emphasizing **inference** alongside training, and earned SemiAnalysis' **Platinum ClusterMAX rating** twice.

**Investor & Analyst Activity:** **Cathie Wood's ARK Invest** purchased ~83,000 shares (~$5.8M). **Coatue Management** (Philippe Laffont) fully exited its CoreWeave position before a significant stock decline, reportedly rotating into another AI stock. **Barclays** maintained a Hold rating with a $90 price target; **Northland Securities** reiterated Buy. The consensus analyst rating is "Moderate Buy." **Stratos Wealth Partners** boosted its position. Insider **Brian Venturo** sold over 1.4 million shares across multiple transactions; CEO **Michael Intrator** also sold shares. CoreWeave's stock is up ~62% over one year (~85% since IPO), though it experienced ~50% decline from October highs.

**Risks:** Heavy customer concentration on **Microsoft**, **OpenAI** (projected $14B loss in 2026), and **Meta**; massive variable-rate debt exposure; potential for rising interest rates; and risk that hyperscaler customers could internalize compute needs.

## Nebius — Emerging Rival

**Nebius Group (NBIS)** stock jumped **15%** on a **$27 billion, five-year AI infrastructure deal with Meta** ($12B dedicated capacity + $15B option). **Nvidia** also took a **$2 billion strategic stake**, helping Nebius deploy 5+ GW of Nvidia systems through 2030. Nebius stock is up **~400% in 12 months**, outpacing CoreWeave's 109%. Q4 revenue surged **547% YoY to $228M**. The company had 170 MW live at end-2025, targeting 800 MW–1 GW by end-2026, and holds a separate deal with **Microsoft** worth up to **$19.4 billion**. It raised $4B in convertible notes and is building a 300 MW data center in Finland. Like CoreWeave, it remains unprofitable with heavy capex.

## Other Cloud/Infrastructure Providers

- **AWS:** Reported fire at a data center in Bahrain following an Iranian attack.
- **Microsoft:** Committed **$1 billion to Thailand's AI future** and **$10 billion to AI and cybersecurity in Japan**. Remains a major CoreWeave customer.
- **Google:** In talks with Poolside as a potential new partner for the Texas data center project after CoreWeave's exit. Launched **Gemma 4** open model family.
- **Nvidia:** Revenue up 65% in fiscal 2026; stock up ~1,360% over 3.5 years. Market cap near $4.1 trillion. P/E ratio of 35. H100 GPU prices surged 40%. Nvidia is a major investor in both CoreWeave (12%+ stake) and Nebius ($2B stake).
- **Iren:** Stock plunged **20% in 5 days**; Freedom Capital issued a Hold rating.
- **Applied Digital (APLD):** Stock jumped on the restructured CoreWeave lease deal; Roth analyst called it a "Top Pick."
- **Cloudflare:** Compared against CoreWeave for AI cloud upside potential.

## Infrastructure Alternatives to CoreWeave

Analysts highlighted **MasTec (MTZ)**, **Vertiv (VRT)**, and **nVent Electric (NVT)** as profitable AI infrastructure plays with sustainable business models, strong backlogs, and lower debt risk compared to CoreWeave's capital-intensive, debt-heavy model. Vertiv posted $10.2B full-year 2025 revenue with 26% organic growth; nVent generated $1B in data center sales (up 50% YoY).
