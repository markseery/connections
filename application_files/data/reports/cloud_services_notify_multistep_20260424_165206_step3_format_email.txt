<h2>Major Customer Deals</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Meta</strong> expanded its commitment to CoreWeave by $21 billion (running 2027–2032), on top of a prior $14.2 billion deal, totaling ~$35 billion. Infrastructure will span multiple locations including NVIDIA Vera Rubin deployments.</li>
<li><strong>Anthropic</strong> signed a multi-year agreement for CoreWeave to power its Claude AI models, pushing CoreWeave's client base to nine of the top ten AI model providers (only xAI excluded).</li>
<li><strong>Jane Street</strong> signed a $6 billion AI cloud agreement with CoreWeave.</li>
<li>CoreWeave now claims an <strong>$88 billion revenue backlog</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Financing &amp; Debt</h2>
<ul>
<li>CoreWeave completed a <strong>$1.0 billion add-on offering of 9.750% Senior Notes due 2031</strong> at 102%, bringing total outstanding notes to $2.75 billion. Proceeds fund general corporate purposes and debt repayment.</li>
<li>Interest expenses surged to $387 million in FQ4 2025, raising concerns about the capital-intensive model. Analysts note relatively low depreciation/sales ratios, questioning whether hardware obsolescence risk is underestimated.</li>
<li><strong>Core Scientific</strong> (formerly a Bitcoin miner) is raising <strong>$3.3 billion in junk bonds</strong> to build six AI data centers leased to CoreWeave under a 12-year, ~$10 billion revenue agreement. Another firm, <strong>Edged Compute</strong>, is marketing $1.3 billion in bonds for facilities leased to CoreWeave and an Alibaba unit.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Cross-Cloud &amp; Google Cloud Partnership</h2>
<ul>
<li>CoreWeave launched <strong>CoreWeave Interconnect</strong> in collaboration with <strong>Google Cloud</strong>, enabling cross-cloud AI training and inference via Google's Partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect. New tools include <strong>SUNK Anywhere</strong> (multi-cloud training across Google Cloud, AWS, Azure, and on-premises) and <strong>LOTA Cross-Cloud</strong> (cross-cloud data/workload flexibility).</li>
<li>This aligns with a broader industry push: <strong>AWS and Google Cloud</strong> launched a joint multicloud interoperability spec in December, with <strong>Microsoft Azure</strong> set to join later in 2026.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Hyperscaler Outlook (Post–Google Cloud Next)</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Jefferies</strong> analysts see <strong>Amazon (AWS), Microsoft (Azure), Alphabet (Google Cloud), Oracle, and CoreWeave</strong> as key AI compute beneficiaries. AI infrastructure demand continues to outstrip supply across GPU generations (Hopper, Blackwell). The industry is shifting toward committed capacity contracts amid persistent GPU scarcity.</li>
<li><strong>Google Cloud</strong> showed the strongest cloud upside skew, followed by AWS and Azure. Microsoft is increasingly evaluated on <strong>Microsoft 365/Copilot</strong> traction rather than Azure beat size alone.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Stock &amp; Insider Activity</h2>
<ul>
<li>CoreWeave's stock surged ~30% in one week, with founders gaining ~$4 billion in paper wealth. Shares are up ~64% despite heavy debt issuance.</li>
<li>Significant <strong>insider selling</strong>: CEO-linked entities sold 307,693 shares ($114–$120/share); insider Brian Venturo sold 1,125,000 shares; a major shareholder sold 319,835 shares.</li>
<li><strong>Institutional buying</strong>: Rakuten Securities, Zurcher Kantonalbank, and Asset Management One all purchased CRWV shares.</li>
<li>Valuation debate is active: the stock trades at ~12.2x sales vs. a 1.8x US IT average; some analysts see it as overvalued at ~$117 vs. a $70 fair value estimate, while bulls cite the massive backlog and potential 10x growth.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Nebius Comparison</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Meta</strong> also locked in up to <strong>$27 billion</strong> in AI capacity with <strong>Nebius</strong>, another neocloud. Motley Fool articles compare CoreWeave and Nebius as competing neocloud investment options, noting Meta is spending ~$48 billion combined between the two.</li>
</ul>

<div class="key-takeaway">
<h2>Key Takeaway</h2>
<p>The AI infrastructure buildout is accelerating at an extraordinary pace, with CoreWeave at the center as a GPU-centric intermediary between NVIDIA and hyperscalers/AI labs. The capital-intensive model carries significant debt and depreciation risks, but massive long-term contracts from Meta, Anthropic, Jane Street, and others—plus cross-cloud partnerships with Google Cloud—position CoreWeave and its peers as critical infrastructure layers in the AI era.</p>
