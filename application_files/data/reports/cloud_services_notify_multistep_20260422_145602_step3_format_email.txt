<tr>
<td style="padding:0 48px 24px;">
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Amazon/AWS &amp; Anthropic</strong> struck a landmark deal: Amazon is investing a fresh <strong>$5 billion</strong> in Anthropic (bringing its total to $13B), and Anthropic committed to spending <strong>over $100 billion on AWS over 10 years</strong>, securing up to 5 GW of compute capacity. The deal centers on Amazon's custom <strong>Trainium2 through Trainium4</strong> chips, with options on future chip generations. Anthropic also separately signed an agreement with <strong>Google and Broadcom</strong> for multiple gigawatts of TPU capacity. VCs are reportedly offering Anthropic capital at an <strong>$800B+ valuation</strong>.
</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Google Cloud</strong> made major moves at <strong>Cloud Next 2026</strong> in Las Vegas. It earmarked a new <strong>$750 million budget</strong> to help partners (from startups to consulting firms) sell AI agents to enterprises—covering Gemini proof-of-concept projects, cloud credits, forward-deployed engineers, and deployment rebates. Google highlighted startups expanding on its cloud, including <strong>Lovable</strong> ($400M ARR track), <strong>Notion</strong> ($11B valuation, using Gemini), <strong>Gamma</strong> ($2.1B valuation, using Nano Banana 2 image model), and <strong>Inferact</strong> (vLLM creators, accessing Nvidia GPUs through Google Cloud).
</p>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
<strong style="color:#0f0f0f;">Google &amp; Thinking Machines Lab</strong> (Mira Murati's startup, formerly OpenAI CTO): A new <strong>multi-billion-dollar deal</strong> (single-digit billions) gives Thinking Machines access to Google Cloud AI infrastructure including systems with <strong>Nvidia GB300 chips</strong> for model training and deployment. This is the startup's first cloud provider deal (non-exclusive). Thinking Machines previously raised a <strong>$2B seed at $12B valuation</strong> and launched its first product, <strong>Tinker</strong>, which automates custom frontier AI model creation using reinforcement learning.
</p>

The partnership leverages SpaceX's Colossus supercomputer (equivalent of 1M Nvidia H100s). This follows xAI renting compute to Cursor and two senior Cursor engineers departing to join xAI under Elon Musk. Cursor's valuation has surged from $2.5B (Jan 2025) → $9B → $29.3B → potentially $50B+ in upcoming rounds.

<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.7;color:#333333;">
Anthropic's powerful cybersecurity model <strong>Mythos</strong> faced an unauthorized access incident: a group accessed it through a <strong>third-party vendor environment</strong>, guessing the model's online location based on Anthropic's naming conventions. The group (from a Discord channel focused on unreleased AI models) demonstrated access to Bloomberg. Mythos was released only to select vendors (including <strong>Apple</strong>) under <strong>Project Glasswing</strong> due to concerns it could be weaponized. Anthropic's CEO also met with the <strong>White House chief of staff</strong> as the US government sought access to Mythos. Sam Altman called Anthropic's restricted release approach <strong>"fear-based marketing"</strong>, arguing it keeps AI in elite hands.
</p>
