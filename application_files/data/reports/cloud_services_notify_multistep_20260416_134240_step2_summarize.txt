## Summary

### AI Agents & Creative Tools
**Canva** updated its AI assistant to plan and execute design tasks using tool-calling, creating editable layered designs from text prompts. This mirrors moves by **Adobe**, which launched its **Firefly AI Assistant** (public beta), capable of orchestrating tasks across Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, and other Creative Cloud apps. **Figma** recently added MCP server support for AI agents. **OpenAI** updated its **Agents SDK** with sandboxing, in-distribution harness for frontier models, and compatibility with sandbox providers to help enterprises build safer, long-horizon agents. Indian startup **Emergent** (backed by **SoftBank**, Khosla, Lightspeed; $70M raise at $300M valuation) launched **Wingman**, an autonomous AI agent operating via WhatsApp/Telegram, competing with tools like OpenClaw and **Anthropic's** Claude agents. **Microsoft**-owned **LinkedIn** is also exploring agent-adjacent AI integrations.

### Cloud & Compute Providers
**Allbirds** (Nasdaq: BIRD), after selling its shoe brand for $39M, rebranded as **NewBird AI**, a "GPU-as-a-Service and AI-native cloud solutions provider," securing $50M in convertible financing to acquire GPU assets—drawing comparisons to Long Island Iced Tea's blockchain pivot. **Parasail** raised a **$32M Series A** to provide cloud inference services, generating 500 billion tokens/day by orchestrating workloads across 40 data centers in 15 countries, competing with proprietary chip owners. The "tokenmaxxing" trend—tracking AI token usage as a productivity proxy—is driving demand for inference compute; **Meta** had an internal tokenmaxxing dashboard before shutting it down. **Reid Hoffman** endorsed the practice. **Maine became the first US state to ban data center construction**, a headwind for cloud infrastructure expansion.

### Anthropic vs. OpenAI Rivalry
**Anthropic's** annualized revenue surged from $9B (end of 2025) to **$30B by March 2026**, largely from coding tools, making its $380B valuation look attractive relative to **OpenAI's** $852B. Some OpenAI investors are expressing doubt, with secondary-market demand for Anthropic shares far outstripping OpenAI's. Anthropic co-founder **Jack Clark** confirmed briefing the **Trump administration** on its new **Mythos** model—deemed too dangerous for public release due to cybersecurity capabilities—while simultaneously suing Trump's DOD over a supply-chain risk designation tied to disputes over military AI use (mass surveillance, autonomous weapons). **UK financial regulators** are rushing to assess Mythos risks. **OpenAI** released a new cybersecurity model to a limited customer group.

### AI Infrastructure & Global Trade
The **AI build-out is powering global goods trade**, with chip-related exports surging. **China's chip tool imports from Southeast Asia** are rising, suggesting supply-chain workarounds amid sanctions. **Google's TurboQuant** algorithm could reduce AI demand for memory chips. **a16z's Martin Casado** argued building AI models isn't as hard as perceived, potentially lowering barriers for new cloud/compute entrants.

### Google
**Google** launched a **native Gemini app for Mac** (Option+Space shortcut), enabling screen sharing, image generation (Nano Banana), and video generation (Veo), catching up to **OpenAI** and **Anthropic** desktop apps.

### Other Notable Developments
- **DeepL** launched real-time voice-to-voice translation with add-ons for **Zoom** and **Microsoft Teams**.
- **Hightouch** reached **$100M ARR**, adding $70M in 20 months via AI-powered marketing content creation for brands.
- **Antioch** raised $8.5M seed ($60M valuation) to build simulation tools bridging the sim-to-real gap for robotics/physical AI.
- **Gitar** emerged from stealth with $9M to deploy AI agents for code validation—addressing "code overload" from vibe coding.
- **Meta** built an AI chatbot version of **Mark Zuckerberg** for internal staff interactions. Big Tech is amassing a **$300M election war chest**.
- **AI chatbots misdiagnose in over 80%** of early medical cases per a new study.
- **LinkedIn** data shows hiring is down ~20% since 2022 but attributes it to interest rates, not AI—yet. Skills needed per job expected to change 70% by 2030.
- **Objection**, a Peter Thiel-backed startup, launched an AI platform to adjudicate journalism accuracy for $2,000 per challenge, raising concerns about chilling investigative reporting.
- **Science Corporation** (Max Hodak, ex-Neuralink) preparing first human brain sensor trials after a $230M Series C at $1.5B valuation.
