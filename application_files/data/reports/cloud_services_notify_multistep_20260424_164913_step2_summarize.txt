## Summary

### Cloud Services & Major Provider Deals

**AWS/Amazon** scored a major deal with **Meta**, which signed on to use millions of **AWS Graviton** ARM-based CPUs to power AI agent workloads (real-time reasoning, code generation, multi-step task coordination). This strategically pulls Meta's spending back toward AWS after Meta's prior $10B six-year deal with **Google Cloud** last August. Meta also uses **Microsoft Azure**. AWS timed the announcement to coincide with the end of **Google Cloud Next**. Separately, **Anthropic** committed $100B over 10 years to run workloads on AWS, focusing on Amazon's **Trainium** custom AI chips, while Amazon invested another $5B into Anthropic (totaling $13B).

**Google** announced sweeping **Workspace** AI updates at Cloud Next, including "Workspace Intelligence" (an AI system drawing on Gmail, Calendar, Chat, Drive), Gemini-powered Google Sheets automation, and AI writing tools for Docs. Google also makes its own custom AI chips and unveiled new versions at the conference. Google is projected to spend $175B–$185B in capex, and is reportedly planning to invest up to **$40B in Anthropic**.

**Microsoft** is offering voluntary redundancy to 7% of its US staff for the first time, amid shifts in its workforce as AI reshapes priorities.

### AI Model Releases & Competition

**DeepSeek** launched preview versions of **V4 Flash** (284B params) and **V4 Pro** (1.6T params — the largest open-weight model available), both mixture-of-experts models with 1M-token context windows. V4-Pro-Max claims to outperform OpenAI's GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3.0 Pro on some reasoning benchmarks and match GPT-5.4 in coding, though it trails GPT-5.4 and Gemini 3.1 Pro on knowledge tests by an estimated 3–6 months. DeepSeek is also targeting a **$20B valuation** to retain staff against poaching. **Cohere and Aleph Alpha** agreed a **$20B transatlantic AI tie-up**.

**OpenAI** released **GPT-5.5**, described as its "smartest and most intuitive" model yet, advancing toward a "super app" combining ChatGPT, Codex, and AI browser. Co-founder Greg Brockman said it delivers more frontier AI for fewer tokens versus GPT-5.4.

### Massive Capex & Infrastructure Spending

**Tesla** raised its 2026 capex plan to **$25B** (3x prior years), driven by AI compute infrastructure, data centers, and robotics. Amazon projects **$200B** in 2026 capex across AI, chips, robotics, and satellites. AI and defense companies dominate US growth investment overall.

### Hardware & GPU Crunch

Apple's **M4 Mac mini** is sold out due to AI demand (running local models like OpenClaw), an industry-wide memory crunch, and planned refreshes — driving eBay markups of $100+ over retail. Astronomers' growing GPU needs (for NASA's Roman telescope, JWST, and Rubin Observatory data analysis) are adding to the **global GPU crunch**. **SK Hynix** (Nvidia supplier) reported another record quarter, citing a "structural shift" in AI-driven memory demand.

### Acquisitions & Funding

**SpaceX** (merged with xAI) preempted Cursor's $2B raise with a **$60B buyout option** (or $10B collaboration payment), bolstering Elon Musk's AI coding ambitions against Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex. **Sierra** (Bret Taylor's customer service AI startup, valued at $10B) acquired YC-backed French startup **Fragment**, its third acquisition. **Era** raised $11M to build a software platform powering next-gen AI gadgets. **Private equity** firms are courting OpenAI and Anthropic. The **UK** is in talks with Anthropic over Mythos access for banks. Anthropic also partnered with **Freshfields** to create legal AI tools.

### Meta Workforce & AI Spending

**Meta** plans to cut **10% of jobs** to "offset" Zuckerberg's massive AI spending.

### Other Notable Developments

- **X** launched Grok-powered Custom Timelines (75+ topics) while shutting down Communities; also exploring "super app" ambitions.
- **Nothing** launched "Essential Voice," a system-level AI dictation tool supporting 100+ languages.
- **Noscroll**, a new startup, offers an AI bot that doomscrolls X for you and texts relevant news.
- The **White House** accused China of "industrial-scale" theft of AI technology, while a Republican senator warned of the "political cost" of supporting AI.
- **Beehiiv** expanded beyond newsletters into webinars, AI podcast analytics, and metered paywalls.
- A security chain-of-failures linked compliance startup **Delve** to breaches at Context AI and **Vercel**.
- India's app market hit $300M in Q1 in-app purchases (+33% YoY), led partly by generative AI apps like ChatGPT.
