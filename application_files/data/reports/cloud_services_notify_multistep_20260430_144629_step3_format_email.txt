<!-- Section 1 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">CoreWeave's Major Deals and Partnerships</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.7;"><strong>CoreWeave</strong> (NASDAQ: CRWV) has been at the center of a flurry of AI infrastructure deal-making:</p>
<ul style="margin:0 0 24px;padding-left:20px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.8;">
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Meta</strong> expanded its commitment to CoreWeave by $21 billion (on top of a prior $14.2 billion arrangement), covering 2027–2032, as Meta supplements its own data center buildout with CoreWeave's GPU capacity. Meta also separately announced a $10 billion Texas data center.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Anthropic</strong> signed a multi-year agreement with CoreWeave to power its Claude AI models, sending CoreWeave shares up ~10%.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Jane Street</strong> signed a $6 billion AI cloud services agreement with CoreWeave.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>OpenAI</strong> remains a key customer. CoreWeave is purchasing GPUs and related components to fulfill OpenAI contracts, funded in part by new debt.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">CoreWeave emphasized its diversified customer base, listing <strong>Meta, Anthropic, Microsoft, Google, IBM, Perplexity AI, Jane Street</strong>, and others.</li>
</ul>

<!-- Section 2 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">Financing and Capital Raises</h2>
<ul style="margin:0 0 24px;padding-left:20px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.8;">
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Morgan Stanley</strong> and <strong>Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group</strong> kicked off a ~$3.1 billion leveraged loan for CoreWeave to buy GPUs, adding to $2.75 billion raised earlier in the month.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">CoreWeave raised $3.5 billion in convertible notes and priced $1 billion of 9.750% senior notes due 2031.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">CoreWeave's SUNK (runtime acceleration) platform expanded capabilities, including "SUNK Anywhere" for multi-cloud environments, aimed at getting AI clusters online faster.</li>
</ul>

<!-- Section 3 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">OpenAI's Missed Targets and Market Fallout</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.7;">A <strong>Wall Street Journal</strong> report revealed that <strong>OpenAI</strong> missed its internal targets for both annual revenue and its goal of 1 billion weekly ChatGPT users by year-end. <strong>Google's Gemini</strong> AI bot grew and ate into OpenAI's market share. This sparked a sell-off in AI infrastructure stocks:</p>
<ul style="margin:0 0 24px;padding-left:20px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.8;">
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Oracle</strong> (ORCL) fell ~4%, <strong>CoreWeave</strong> ~5%, and <strong>AMD</strong> and <strong>Nvidia</strong> also declined.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Oracle</strong> defended its OpenAI partnership on X, expressing continued excitement. CoreWeave noted OpenAI is "a terrific partner, but not our only one."</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">Analysts warned that OpenAI's slowing growth should still concern <strong>Oracle</strong> and <strong>CoreWeave</strong> investors given their heavy spending on AI capacity.</li>
</ul>

<!-- Section 4 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">Nebius</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.7;"><strong>Nebius Group</strong> (NasdaqGS: NBIS) is in active talks to acquire Israeli AI firm <strong>AI21 Labs</strong> (after Nvidia exited earlier negotiations). Nebius is also expanding its AI infrastructure through new data center capacity contracts with <strong>CoreWeave</strong>. The company positions itself as a full-stack AI cloud competitor to <strong>AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud</strong>. Nebius shares were up 61% YTD and ~7x over one year, trading at ~$145.</p>

<!-- Section 5 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">Galaxy Digital and Data Centers</h2>
<p style="margin:0 0 24px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.7;"><strong>Galaxy Digital</strong> narrowed its Q1 net loss to $216 million and delivered its first data hall at its Helios campus in Texas to <strong>CoreWeave</strong>, beginning revenue under a long-term AI workload lease. Galaxy secured approval to expand Helios power capacity to over 1.6 GW.</p>

<!-- Section 6 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">Analyst Activity and Stock Movements</h2>
<ul style="margin:0 0 24px;padding-left:20px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.8;">
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Wells Fargo</strong> reiterated CoreWeave as overweight, raising its price target to $135 from $125, calling it the leading "pick-and-shovel" AI infrastructure play.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Vanguard Group</strong> purchased CoreWeave shares; <strong>Cathie Wood</strong> (ARK Invest) was reported buying CoreWeave, Spotify, and Google.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">CoreWeave insiders sold significant stock (~$40.9 million and 200,000 shares in separate transactions).</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">CoreWeave's stock showed extreme volatility—down over 60% from its $187 high at its worst, then rallying 61% from late-March lows. It traded around $105–$112 in late April.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">The company reports Q1 2026 earnings on May 7.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">Valuation debates intensified: CoreWeave has a 47.5% EBITDA margin and ~$5.1 billion revenue but remains net-income negative (EPS of -$0.56). Analysts are split on whether it is overvalued or a growth opportunity.</li>
</ul>

<!-- Section 7 -->
<h2 style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;color:#0f172a;border-bottom:2px solid #e2e8f0;padding-bottom:12px;">Broader Cloud/AI Competition Context</h2>
<ul style="margin:0 0 32px;padding-left:20px;font-size:15px;color:#334155;line-height:1.8;">
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Google's</strong> Gemini is gaining ground against ChatGPT.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;"><strong>Microsoft, Google, Meta</strong>, and other hyperscalers continue building their own AI data centers while also relying on neocloud providers like CoreWeave and Nebius for supplemental capacity.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom:10px;">Concerns persist about AI sector overspending and whether demand will justify the massive infrastructure buildout.</li>
</ul>
