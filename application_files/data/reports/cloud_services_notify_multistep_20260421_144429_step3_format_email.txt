<p style="margin:0 0 14px;font-size:15px;line-height:1.65;color:#333333;">Anthropic's <strong>Mythos Preview</strong>, a frontier cybersecurity model deemed too capable for public release, has been restricted to ~40 organizations. Amodei has publicly stated he doesn't want AI "turned on our own people."</p>
<p style="margin:0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.65;color:#333333;">The UK government warned that companies "should be worried" about Mythos's cybersecurity implications. Multiple FT analyses frame Mythos as testing the limits of global cyber defenses and signaling new "economics of AI scarcity."</p>
