# CoreWeave Dominates AI Infrastructure Headlines with Landmark Deals

## Summary

**CoreWeave** (CRWV) had a blockbuster week in early-mid April 2026, securing multiple massive AI infrastructure contracts and financing deals that sent its stock surging ~24% for the week.

### Key Deals

- **Meta Platforms** expanded its partnership with CoreWeave by $21 billion (running 2027–2032), on top of a prior $14.2 billion commitment, bringing Meta's total to over $35 billion. The infrastructure will include early deployments of the **NVIDIA Vera Rubin** platform and support Meta's inference workloads.

- **Anthropic** signed a multi-year agreement for CoreWeave to provide cloud computing capacity to develop and deploy its **Claude AI models**, with capacity coming online later this year using various NVIDIA chip architectures. Financial terms were not disclosed. CoreWeave's stock jumped ~11-12% on this news alone.

- CoreWeave's **OpenAI** contract also grew to $22.4 billion (up from an initial $11.9 billion). Combined, Meta and OpenAI represent nearly two-thirds of CoreWeave's guaranteed revenue backlog.

- CoreWeave now serves **9 of the top 10 AI model providers**, with Elon Musk's **xAI** being the only holdout.

### Financing & Capital Markets

CoreWeave demonstrated aggressive capital formation alongside its deals:
- Raised ~$3.5 billion in **convertible senior notes**
- Secured an **$8.5 billion delayed draw term loan** at investment-grade ratings (its first)
- Upsized a **high-yield bond offering** due to heavy demand
- Revenue backlog reached **$66.8–$87.8 billion**
- 2026 revenue guidance of $12–13 billion (up ~100% YoY)

CFO Nitin Agrawal described it as one of the largest dual-tranche raises of its kind. Morningstar analyst Luke Yang noted that creditors are growing increasingly comfortable with "neocloud" business models, with sequential improvements in credit ratings and interest rates.

### Analyst Activity

- **Macquarie** upgraded CoreWeave from Neutral to **Outperform**, raising its price target from $90 to $125 (~40% upside), citing CoreWeave's structural importance and near-term capacity advantages over rival cloud providers.
- **Citizens** projected up to **96% upside** potential.
- **Murphy** (unnamed firm) maintained a Neutral/$90 target but acknowledged the Meta deal could de-risk long-term revenue.
- Simply Wall St's most popular narrative values CoreWeave at ~$133 (23.4% undervalued vs. ~$102 share price).

### Risks & Concerns

- CoreWeave spends approximately **$2.60 for every $1 of revenue** generated, with $30–35 billion in capex spending planned
- **$21 billion in long-term debt** and negative free cash flow
- High **customer concentration** (Meta ~40% of backlog)
- Power availability and interconnect capacity constraints remain industry-wide risks
- Competition from hyperscalers (**AWS, Azure, Google**) with virtually unlimited resources, plus specialized competitors like **Lambda Labs**
- Hardware obsolescence risk as NVIDIA chip generations turn over rapidly

### Broader Context

- **Anthropic** separately signed a deal with **Google** and **Broadcom** for ~3.5 GW of computing capacity built on Google's TPUs (coming online 2027). Anthropic's annualized revenue surpassed $30 billion.
- **Meta** announced plans to spend $115–135 billion on AI infrastructure in 2026, nearly double 2025 levels. It also invested $10 billion in a Texas data center and launched its **Superintelligence Labs** unit.
- **NVIDIA** made a $2 billion investment in CoreWeave in early 2026, reinforcing confidence in the platform.
- **Google** expanded its partnership with **Intel** for AI chips.
- **OpenAI** halted its UK Stargate project amid regulatory and energy price concerns.
- The broader market saw geopolitical pressure (U.S. Strait of Hormuz blockade threat, Iran negotiations collapse) weighing on risk assets, causing CoreWeave to dip in pre-market on April 13 despite the bullish fundamentals.
