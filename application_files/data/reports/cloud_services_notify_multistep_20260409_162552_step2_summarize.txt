## Summary

### AWS & Amazon's Custom Chips Challenge Nvidia
Amazon CEO Andy Jassy's annual shareholder letter positioned AWS's homegrown **Trainium** AI chips as a formidable alternative to Nvidia. He claims Trainium has reached a **$20 billion annual revenue run rate**, with capacity for Trainium3 nearly sold out and Trainium4 (18 months away) also nearly committed. Jassy argues that if Amazon sold chips externally, it would be at a $50B ARR — though Nvidia still did $215.9B in actual revenue last year. Separately, **Uber** expanded its AWS contract to use more **Graviton** CPUs and begin trialing **Trainium3**, a notable shift given Uber's previous major cloud commitments to **Oracle** and **Google Cloud**.

### AWS Navigates Dual Investments in Anthropic & OpenAI
AWS CEO Matt Garman addressed Amazon's **$50 billion investment in OpenAI** alongside its existing **$8 billion in Anthropic**, calling it a manageable conflict given AWS's long history of competing with its own partners. He noted this is industry-wide: Anthropic's latest $30B round included investors also backing OpenAI, including **Microsoft**.

### Anthropic: New Model, Massive Compute Expansion, and UK Courtship
**Anthropic** previewed its powerful new frontier model **Mythos** through **Project Glasswing**, a cybersecurity initiative with 12 partners including **Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike**, and others. Mythos has reportedly identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities. Separately, Anthropic signed a **major new compute deal with Google Cloud and Broadcom** for expanded TPU capacity (reportedly **3.5 gigawatts**), building on an earlier 1+ GW agreement, with new capacity coming online in 2027. This supports Anthropic's $50B U.S. infrastructure commitment. The company recently closed a **$30B Series G at a $380B valuation**. The **UK is also courting Anthropic** to expand in London following Anthropic's clash with the U.S. Defense Department, which labeled it a supply-chain risk.

### OpenAI Halts Stargate UK Data Centre Project
**OpenAI has halted its Stargate UK data centre project**, according to the Financial Times. Details are behind a paywall, but it signals a pullback on international infrastructure expansion.

### Google Cloud & Google Products
Beyond the Anthropic TPU deal, **Google** quietly launched **"Google AI Edge Eloquent"**, an offline-first AI dictation app on iOS using Gemma-based models, competing with Wispr Flow and others. **Google Maps** added Gemini-powered AI caption generation for user-contributed photos.

### Firmus: Nvidia-Backed Data Center Builder Soars
Singapore-based **Firmus** raised **$505 million at a $5.5B valuation** (led by Coatue), bringing total funding to $1.35B in six months. The company, originally a Bitcoin mining cooling provider, is building **Nvidia Vera Rubin-powered "AI factory" data centers** in Australia and Tasmania (Project Southgate), using Nvidia's reference designs.

### Intel Joins Musk's Terafab Chips Project
**Intel** signed on to Elon Musk's **Terafab** semiconductor factory project in Texas, joining SpaceX and Tesla. Intel will serve as the manufacturing partner, bringing critical fab expertise that SpaceX and Tesla lack. The facility aims to produce 1 TW/year of compute for AI and robotics. Intel's stock rose ~3% on the news.

### Meta Releases New AI Model
**Meta** released its first AI model since Zuckerberg's major spending spree, per the FT (details paywalled).

### Databricks Hits Milestones
Databricks CTO **Matei Zaharia** won the **ACM Prize in Computing**. Databricks has raised over $20B at a $134B valuation and hit $5.4B revenue run rate. Zaharia declared "AGI is here already."

### Atlassian Adds AI Agents to Confluence
**Atlassian** launched visual AI tool **Remix** and three third-party agents (connecting to **Lovable, Replit, and Gamma**) within Confluence, using model context protocols.

### Startups & Other Developments
- **Poke** raised $10M more (valued at $300M), offering AI agents via iMessage/SMS/WhatsApp, backed by Spark Capital and General Catalyst.
- **Arcee**, a 26-person startup, released a 400B-parameter open-source reasoning model on a $20M budget, positioning as a Western alternative to Chinese models.
- **Astropad** launched **Workbench**, a remote desktop tool for monitoring AI agents on Mac Minis.
- **OpenAI** released a **Child Safety Blueprint** with NCMEC to combat AI-generated child exploitation.
- **Tubi** became the first streamer with a native app inside **ChatGPT**.
- **Samsung** forecasts record profit driven by the AI boom.
- **Bezos's Project Prometheus** poached an xAI co-founder from OpenAI.
- Insurers are turning to **catastrophe bonds** to offload data centre risks.
- **Arm** chief Haas is in line to lead much of **SoftBank's** international business.
- Top record labels and **Suno** hit an impasse in AI-generated music licensing talks.
