<p style="margin:20px 0 0 0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.75;color:#334155;">
<strong style="color:#0f172a;">CoreWeave</strong> (NASDAQ: CRWV) and <strong style="color:#0f172a;">Meta Platforms</strong> (META) announced a massively expanded AI infrastructure agreement worth approximately <strong style="color:#0f172a;">$21 billion</strong>, building on a prior deal valued at $14.2 billion and bringing total commitments to over $35 billion. The long-term contract runs through <strong style="color:#0f172a;">December 2032</strong> and will provide Meta with dedicated AI cloud capacity deployed across multiple locations, including some of the <strong style="color:#0f172a;">first deployments of NVIDIA's next-generation Vera Rubin platform</strong> — a supercomputing architecture expected to enable the shift from generative AI to "agentic AI."
</p>
<p style="margin:16px 0 0 0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.75;color:#334155;">
A key focus of the deal is <strong style="color:#0f172a;">AI inference</strong> (running trained models at scale in real time), reflecting an industry-wide pivot from model training to continuous production deployment. CoreWeave CEO <strong style="color:#0f172a;">Michael Intrator</strong> stated this demonstrates that "leading companies are choosing CoreWeave's AI cloud to run their most demanding workloads."
</p>

<p style="margin:20px 0 0 0;font-size:15px;line-height:1.75;color:#334155;">
CoreWeave, founded in 2017 and originally a cryptocurrency mining operation, pivoted to enterprise-grade NVIDIA GPU cloud infrastructure in 2019. It operates a <strong style="color:#0f172a;">"specialized cloud" model</strong> focused exclusively on high-performance compute (AI training, inference, VFX rendering), differentiating itself from general-purpose hyperscalers like <strong style="color:#0f172a;">AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud</strong>. Key clients include <strong style="color:#0f172a;">Meta, OpenAI, Alphabet/Google, Microsoft, Mistral, and Anthropic</strong>. The company reports a backlog exceeding <strong style="color:#0f172a;">$66 billion</strong> and has earned SemiAnalysis' Platinum ClusterMAX™ rating twice.
</p>
