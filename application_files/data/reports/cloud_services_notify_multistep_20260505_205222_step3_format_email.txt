<h2 style="margin:0 0 24px;font-size:22px;font-weight:700;color:#0f0f0f;letter-spacing:-0.3px;">AI Infrastructure, Cloud Services &amp; Chip Makers</h2>

<p style="margin:0 0 20px;font-size:15px;color:#333333;">
<strong>ASML</strong> CEO Christophe Fouquet affirmed the Dutch lithography monopoly's unchallenged position, noting that with <strong>Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, and Google</strong> committing over $600 billion in AI infrastructure spending this year, demand for ASML's EUV machines has surged beyond supply. A Peter Thiel-backed startup called Substrate has raised $1B+ claiming it can rival ASML, while reports of Chinese reverse-engineering efforts persist — but Fouquet remains unfazed.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Source: <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/asml-ceo-christophe-fouquet-no-one-is-coming-for-us/" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">TechCrunch</a></span>
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 20px;font-size:15px;color:#333333;">
<strong>Krutrim</strong>, India's first GenAI unicorn, is pivoting from AI model development to <strong>cloud services</strong> after pausing chip design efforts, conducting layoffs, and pulling its AI assistant app. The startup generated ~$31.5M in revenue in FY2026 but acknowledged the tough economics of building large-scale AI systems, contrasting with rivals like Sarvam that continue shipping models and partnerships.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Source: <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/indias-first-genai-unicorn-shifts-to-cloud-services-as-ai-model-ambitions-face-reality/" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">TechCrunch</a></span>
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 20px;font-size:15px;color:#333333;">
<strong>Cerebras Systems</strong>, an AI chipmaker offering the Wafer-Scale Engine 3 chip as an alternative to GPUs, is preparing a blockbuster IPO — selling 28 million shares at $115–$125/share to raise $3.5B at a ~$26.6B market cap. Key investors include OpenAI's Sam Altman and Greg Brockman, Benchmark, Fidelity, Tiger Global, Coatue, and Abu Dhabi's G42. This would be the largest tech IPO of 2026.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Source: <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/openais-cozy-partner-cerebras-is-on-track-for-a-blockbuster-ipo/" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">TechCrunch</a></span>
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 20px;font-size:15px;color:#333333;">
<strong>Peter Thiel</strong> is backing a $1B ocean data center startup powered by wave energy, signaling novel approaches to AI infrastructure challenges.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Source: <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/711ce313-16fb-4a12-b6be-fbed547c8a39" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">Financial Times</a></span>
</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 20px;font-size:15px;color:#333333;">
<strong>Banks</strong> are seeking to offload risk to avoid "choking" on data center debt, reflecting the enormous capital demands of AI infrastructure buildout.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Source: <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/08aba5e4-5834-4e79-a48d-989a2c5bad0f" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">Financial Times</a></span>
</p>

<strong>Anthropic</strong> announced a $1.5B joint venture for enterprise AI services with <strong>Blackstone</strong>, <strong>Hellman &amp; Friedman</strong>, and <strong>Goldman Sachs</strong>, along with Apollo, General Atlantic, GIC, and Sequoia Capital. Simultaneously, <strong>OpenAI</strong> is raising $4B for a parallel venture called "The Development Company" at a $10B valuation, backed by TPG, Brookfield, Advent, and Bain Capital. Both ventures aim to deploy forward-deployed engineers and capture enterprise AI deal flow through investors' portfolio companies.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Sources: <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/anthropic-and-openai-are-both-launching-joint-ventures-for-enterprise-ai-services/" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">TechCrunch</a>, <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/01b31619-66ff-4a9d-8927-533296ae2b51" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">Financial Times</a></span>

<strong>Sierra</strong> (led by OpenAI chairman Bret Taylor) raised $950M led by Tiger Global and GV at a $15B+ valuation, claiming 40%+ of the Fortune 50 as customers for its AI-powered customer experience platform, with ARR growing from $100M to $150M in under three months. Uber's CTO noted the company "blew through" its AI budget but is seeing ~10% of code generated autonomously.
<br><span style="font-size:13px;color:#888;">Source: <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/" style="color:#555;text-decoration:underline;">TechCrunch</a></span>
