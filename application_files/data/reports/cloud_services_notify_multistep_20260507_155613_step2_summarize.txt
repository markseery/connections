## Summary

### Cloud & Compute Infrastructure

**xAI becomes a cloud provider ("neocloud"):** xAI and Anthropic announced a major partnership in which Anthropic is buying all compute capacity (~300MW) at xAI's Colossus 1 data center, likely worth billions. Musk explained xAI had migrated training to its newer Colossus 2 facility. This effectively turns xAI from a compute consumer into a cloud provider, monetizing its infrastructure and bolstering its balance sheet ahead of an expected IPO (combined with SpaceX, valued ~$1.25 trillion). The deal also lends credibility to SpaceX's orbital data center ambitions.

**SpaceX "Terafab" chip factory:** SpaceX is considering spending $55–119 billion on a vertically integrated semiconductor fabrication facility in Grimes County, Texas, partnering with Intel. The factory would produce chips for AI servers, satellites, orbital data centers, autonomous Tesla vehicles, and robots. Musk argues existing chip manufacturers can't keep pace with his companies' AI and robotics needs.

**Google Cloud's explosive growth and supply constraints:** Google Cloud revenue crossed $20 billion last quarter (up 63%), while its committed backlog nearly doubled in one quarter from $250 billion to $460 billion. ASML CEO Christophe Fouquet warned the chip market will remain "supply limited" for 2–5 years, meaning hyperscalers (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta) won't get all the chips they need. Sundar Pichai admitted Google Cloud revenue was lower than possible because the company was "capacity constrained."

**AI hyperscaler earnings anomaly:** The FT flagged a mysterious $53 billion in "other income" boosting AI hyperscaler earnings, raising questions about the financial underpinnings of the cloud/AI boom.

**Google, xAI, and Microsoft** agreed to US national security reviews of new AI models.

### AI Model Companies & Fundraising

**Moonshot AI (China) raises $2B at $20B valuation:** The Beijing-based lab behind the popular open-weight Kimi series of LLMs raised ~$2 billion, led by Meituan's VC arm, bringing total recent fundraising to $3.9 billion. Its Kimi K2.6 is the second-most-used LLM on OpenRouter, with ARR exceeding $200 million.

**DeepSeek nears $45B valuation:** The Chinese AI lab is raising its first venture round, with valuation jumping from $20B to $45B in weeks. Led by China's state semiconductor investment fund, with Tencent and Alibaba reportedly in talks. DeepSeek is raising primarily to offer employee equity to prevent researcher poaching, and has optimized its models to run on Huawei chips.

**Anthropic** secured massive compute capacity through the xAI/Colossus 1 deal, immediately raising its usage limits. The FT also explored whether Anthropic are "really the good guys."

### AI Products & Applications

**Spotify** is positioning itself as the home for AI-generated personal audio, launching a CLI tool (beta) that lets users create AI podcasts via coding agents (OpenAI Codex, Claude Code, etc.) and import them to Spotify. Separately, Spotify's AI DJ expanded to French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese, now available in 75+ countries.

**Google** updated AI search (AI Overviews) to include quotes/excerpts from Reddit, web forums, and blogs, plus highlighting links from users' news subscriptions—though accuracy concerns persist.

**Meta** is planning an advanced "agentic" AI assistant for consumers, and was sued by publishers over "massive" copyright infringement related to AI training.

**Apple** agreed to pay $250 million to settle a class action over marketing AI features (especially Siri upgrades) that weren't delivered at iPhone 15/16 launch. The upgraded Siri, rumored to be powered by Google Gemini or multiple third-party LLMs, is expected to be previewed at WWDC.

### AI Industry Dynamics

**Snap–Perplexity deal collapses:** Snap revealed its $400M integration deal with Perplexity AI "amicably ended" in Q1 after the companies couldn't agree on a broader rollout path.

**Match Group** is slowing hiring to fund enterprise AI tool subscriptions for employees, aiming to become "AI-native."

**Arm** projects $2 billion in sales from its new AI chip starting next year.

**SoftBank shares surged** as the Japanese stock market hit a record high, buoyed by AI investment optimism. **JPMorgan and BlackRock** leaders played down talk of an AI bubble.

**Barry Diller** commented that "trust is irrelevant" as AGI approaches—the consequences of AI are unknown even to its creators.

**OpenAI–Musk trial:** Greg Brockman testified about Musk's 2017 demand for full control of OpenAI, his angry departure when refused, and subsequent withdrawal of funding. Musk had also tried to recruit Altman for a role at Tesla before the falling out.

**Aurora Innovation** is scaling commercial driverless trucking from a handful to hundreds of trucks, arguing long-haul trucking will crack the autonomy business case before robotaxis.
