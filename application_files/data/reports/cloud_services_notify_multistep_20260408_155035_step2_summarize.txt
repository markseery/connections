## Summary

### Cloud Infrastructure & Data Centers

**Anthropic** signed a major expanded compute deal with **Google Cloud** and **Broadcom** for increased TPU (tensor processing unit) capacity, building on an October 2025 agreement for over 1 GW of compute. A Broadcom SEC filing reveals the new deal includes 3.5 GW of compute, coming online in 2027, as part of Anthropic's $50 billion commitment to U.S. compute infrastructure. Anthropic recently closed a $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion valuation.

**Uber** expanded its **AWS** cloud contract, increasing use of AWS's Graviton CPUs and beginning trials of **Trainium3**, Amazon's AI chip competing with Nvidia. This represents a notable shift, as Uber had previously signed major multi-year cloud deals with **Oracle** and **Google Cloud** in 2023 to migrate from on-premise data centers.

**Firmus**, a Singapore-based AI data center provider backed by **Nvidia**, raised $505 million led by Coatue at a $5.5 billion valuation, bringing its total to $1.35 billion in six months. Firmus is building energy-efficient "AI factory" data centers in Australia (Project Southgate) using Nvidia's Vera Rubin next-gen platform, succeeding Blackwell architecture.

**Iran** threatened to attack **Stargate** data centers (the $500 billion OpenAI/SoftBank/Oracle joint venture) in the UAE, as well as other tech infrastructure in the Middle East. Iranian missiles had already struck **AWS data centers in Bahrain** and an **Oracle data center in Dubai**. Iran also threatened companies including Nvidia and Apple by name.

**Intel** joined Elon Musk's **Terafab** semiconductor project alongside SpaceX and Tesla, aiming to build a new U.S. chip fab in Texas. Intel will serve as the manufacturing partner for the facility targeting 1 TW/year of AI compute. Intel's stock rose ~3% on the news.

**Poolside**, an AI startup, is hunting for new data center partners after a deal with **CoreWeave** fell through (FT, paywalled).

Insurers are turning to **catastrophe bonds** to offload growing data center risks amid the AI infrastructure boom.

### AI Models & Products

**Anthropic** previewed its new frontier model **Mythos** as part of "Project Glasswing," a cybersecurity initiative with 12 partners including **Amazon, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, and Palo Alto Networks**. Mythos identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities in software systems, many one to two decades old. The model is not generally available. Anthropic remains in a legal battle with the Trump administration after the Pentagon labeled it a supply-chain risk.

**Microsoft** launched a "mid-class" AI model as compute limits constrain frontier model deployment (FT, paywalled).

**Google** quietly released "**Google AI Edge Eloquent**," an offline-first AI dictation app on iOS using Gemma-based speech recognition models, with cloud Gemini integration optional. Google Maps also added **Gemini-powered photo captions** for community contributions.

**Arcee**, a 26-person U.S. startup, released its **Trinity Large Thinking** reasoning model — a 400B-parameter open-weight LLM built on a $20 million budget — positioning it as a Western alternative to Chinese open-source models.

**Databricks** co-founder Matei Zaharia won the 2026 **ACM Prize in Computing** for his contributions (including Apache Spark). He declared "AGI is here already" and urged rethinking human standards for AI. Databricks is valued at $134 billion with $5.4 billion in revenue.

### AI Safety, Policy & Economy

**OpenAI** released a **Child Safety Blueprint** developed with NCMEC and state attorneys general, addressing AI-generated child sexual abuse material (up 14% in early 2025 per IWF). The blueprint focuses on updating legislation, improving reporting to law enforcement, and integrating safeguards into AI systems.

**OpenAI** also published economic policy proposals advocating public wealth funds, robot taxes, shifting tax burden from labor to capital, and a four-day workweek to manage AI-driven economic transformation. Separately, OpenAI acquired a popular tech talk show for "low hundreds of millions."

**ChatGPT** rolled out app integrations with DoorDash, Spotify, Uber, Angi, Booking.com, and others, allowing users to connect accounts and take actions directly through the chatbot.

### AI Investment Trends

Family offices and private wealth are increasingly investing directly in AI startups, bypassing traditional VC funds. Arena Private Wealth co-led a $230 million round into AI chip startup **Positron**. A new VC fund called **Zero Shot**, founded by OpenAI alumni, made its first close targeting $100 million.

**Samsung** forecast record profits driven by the AI boom. The AI gold rush is broadly pulling private wealth into riskier, earlier-stage bets.

### Enterprise AI

**Atlassian** launched visual AI tool **Remix** (open beta) in Confluence, plus three third-party agents using MCPs connecting to **Lovable**, **Replit**, and **Gamma** for prototyping, app building, and presentations.

Indian startup **Rocket** launched a platform generating McKinsey-style consulting reports using AI, targeting the "what to build" gap before vibe-coding.

**Bezos's Project Prometheus** poached an xAI co-founder from OpenAI (FT, paywalled). The UK is courting Anthropic to expand in London after its U.S. defense clash, and is also considering testing AI models used by banks.
