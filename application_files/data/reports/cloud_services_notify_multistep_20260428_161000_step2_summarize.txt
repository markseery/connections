## Summary

### OpenAI–Microsoft Partnership Restructured
OpenAI and **Microsoft** renegotiated their partnership, replacing Microsoft's exclusive access to OpenAI IP with a **nonexclusive license through 2032**. Microsoft remains OpenAI's "primary cloud partner," and OpenAI committed to buying $250B more of **Azure** cloud services. Critically, **OpenAI can now serve products across any cloud provider**, resolving potential legal conflict stemming from OpenAI's up-to-$50B deal with **Amazon**. Products will still ship "first on Azure" unless Microsoft cannot support the required capabilities.

### Google Cloud & AI Expansion
- **Google** is banking on its AI capabilities to close the gap with cloud rivals **Amazon (AWS)** and **Microsoft (Azure)**, per FT reporting.
- **Google** signed a **multibillion-dollar cloud deal with Thinking Machines Lab (TML)**, giving the AI startup access to Nvidia's latest GB300 chips — placing TML in the same infrastructure tier as Anthropic and Meta.
- **Google** is planning to invest **up to $40 billion in Anthropic**, further deepening its AI cloud partnerships.
- YouTube (Google) is testing **"Ask YouTube,"** an AI-powered interactive search feature delivering step-by-step, mixed text-and-video answers, available first to Premium subscribers in the U.S.
- **Google employees** are urging their CEO to block U.S. military AI use.

### Amazon & Data Center Energy
- **Amazon-backed nuclear developer X-energy** surged 27% in its trading debut, reflecting growing investor interest in nuclear power for data centers.
- **Jeff Bezos's AI lab** is in talks to take London office space at King's Cross, signaling expansion.

### Meta's AI & Infrastructure Moves
- **China's NDRC blocked Meta's ~$2B acquisition of Manus**, an agentic AI startup founded by Chinese engineers (now Singapore-based). Manus employees had already integrated into Meta's offices; founders are reportedly under Chinese exit bans.
- **Meta** signed a deal with **Overview Energy** to potentially deploy ~1,000 satellites beaming infrared solar power to data centers at night, addressing its massive (18,000+ GWh) electricity needs.
- **Meta** is losing and gaining AI talent in a bidding war with **Thinking Machines Lab**, which has poached key researchers including PyTorch co-founder Soumith Chintala.

### Data Center Policy & Infrastructure
- **Maine's governor vetoed** what would have been the first statewide data center moratorium in the U.S., citing a locally supported project.
- The **"great American data centre divide"** is emerging as a major tension between tech expansion and local community concerns.
- **Investors are pushing for higher yields on $14B of Oracle-backed data center debt**, reflecting financing pressures in the sector.
- The **UK admitted that AI data center emissions are vastly underestimated**.

### Sovereign AI & Consolidation
- **Cohere** (Canada, valued at ~$6.8B) is acquiring **Aleph Alpha** (Germany) to create a sovereign AI alternative to U.S. giants. **Schwarz Group** (operator of the **STACKIT** sovereign cloud platform) is backing the deal with €500M and leading Cohere's Series E at a ~$20B valuation. The combined entity will run on STACKIT's cloud infrastructure.

### OpenAI Hardware & Other Developments
- **OpenAI** may be developing a smartphone in collaboration with **MediaTek, Qualcomm, and Luxshare**, potentially replacing traditional apps with AI agents. Mass production could begin in 2028.
- OpenAI CEO Sam Altman **apologized** to Tumbler Ridge, Canada, after the company failed to alert law enforcement about a flagged ChatGPT user involved in a mass shooting.

### Major AI Funding
- **Ineffable Intelligence** (founded by ex-DeepMind researcher David Silver) raised **$1.1B at $5.1B valuation** to build reinforcement-learning-based AI that learns without human data.
- **ComfyUI** raised $30M at a $500M valuation for its node-based AI media generation tool (4M+ users).

### Enterprise & Developer Tools
- **Red Hat's** OpenClaw maintainer released **Tank OS**, an open-source tool for safer enterprise deployment of OpenClaw AI agents using Podman containers.
- **Otter** launched enterprise search via **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, connecting Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Jira, Salesforce, and soon **Microsoft Outlook, Teams, SharePoint**, and Slack.

### Other Notable Items
- **Anthropic** ran "Project Deal," a test marketplace for agent-on-agent commerce where AI agents negotiated real deals.
- A Bay Area homeowner is seeking to trade a property for **Anthropic equity**.
- **Apple** named John Ternus as incoming CEO, signaling a hardware-focused AI strategy.
- The **White House** accused China of "industrial-scale" theft of AI technology.
- UK tech ministers warned that Starmer's EU reset could "smother" UK innovation.
