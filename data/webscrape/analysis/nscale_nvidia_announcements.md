Nscale leads with dramatic performance multipliers relative to previous generations or competitors:

- **"4X FASTER LLM Training"** — NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 delivers 4× faster training for large language models at scale

- **"~33% latency reduction"** — Updated benchmarks showcasing inference latency improvements

## 2. **Energy Efficiency & Sustainability**

Sustainability is a signature differentiator woven into every hardware announcement:
