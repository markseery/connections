# Benefits CoreWeave Typically Highlights When Announcing New NVIDIA Chips/Systems

When CoreWeave announces the availability of a new NVIDIA chip or system, its messaging follows a consistent and well-defined pattern, combining several key benefit themes. Below is a comprehensive, deduplicated synthesis with specific examples drawn from across CoreWeave's announcements and website content.

---

## 1. First-to-Market / Early Availability

CoreWeave consistently positions itself as the **first cloud provider** to deploy or offer new NVIDIA technology:

- **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72:** "CoreWeave Becomes First Cloud Provider to Deploy NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 Platform"
- **NVIDIA GB200 NVL72:** "CoreWeave Becomes the First Cloud Provider with Generally Available NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 Instances"
- **NVIDIA H200:** "CoreWeave is the First Cloud Provider to Deploy NVIDIA H200 Tensor Core GPUs"
- **NVIDIA HGX H100:** NovelAI described as "Among the First to Have NVIDIA HGX H100 GPUs Online" on CoreWeave
- **NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition:** "CoreWeave Becomes the First AI Cloud Provider to Offer NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU at Scale"
- **NVIDIA A40:** "CoreWeave Deploys the Largest Fleet of NVIDIA A40 GPUs in North America"
- **NVIDIA HGX B300:** "Now generally available" prominently featured

This "first mover" positioning signals that customers can access cutting-edge hardware faster through CoreWeave than through competitors.

## 2. Performance at Scale

CoreWeave repeatedly emphasizes that its infrastructure delivers the full performance potential of NVIDIA hardware **at scale**, not just on individual nodes:

- **NVIDIA HGX B300:** "CoreWeave unlocks the NVIDIA HGX B300 performance at scale for agentic AI"
- **NVIDIA GB300 NVL72:** "More than 6× performance gain on DeepSeek-R1"
- **NVIDIA GB200 Blackwell Cluster (MLPerf):** "Achieving Over 2× Faster Training" with the largest NVIDIA GB200 Blackwell cluster
- **GPU Cluster Efficiency:** "CoreWeave Drives 20% Higher GPU Cluster Performance" — claiming up to 20% higher GPU cluster performance than alternatives
- **Goodput:** CoreWeave highlights achievable **goodput of up to 96%**, signaling efficient, usable compute
- **MFU:** Up to **20% higher Model FLOPS Utilization (MFU)** compared to alternatives
- **Mistral AI / GB200 NVL72:** **2.5× faster training speeds** compared to previous generations
