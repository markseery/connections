# Benefits IREN Typically Highlights When Announcing NVIDIA Chips, Systems, or Products

Based on a comprehensive review of IREN's website content, when IREN announces or discusses the availability of new NVIDIA hardware (such as H100, H200, B200, B300, or GB300 NVL72 systems), it consistently highlights the following categories of benefits:

---

## 1. **Massive Scale and GPU Fleet Size**
- Growth to **over 23,000 GPUs** spanning multiple NVIDIA architectures (H100 through GB300)
- Sweetwater campus capable of supporting **over 700,000 liquid-cooled Blackwell GPUs**
- Expansion of AI Cloud capacity to **150,000 GPUs**
- Delivery of **large-scale GPU clusters** (e.g., the $9.7B Microsoft agreement powered by NVIDIA GB300 GPUs)

## 2. **Latest / Next-Generation Architecture Leadership**
- Emphasis on deploying **"the latest NVIDIA accelerated computing architecture"** — specifically calling out the **Blackwell architecture**
- Naming specific product tiers (HGX B300, HGX B200, GB300 NVL72) to signal cutting-edge positioning
- Infrastructure described as **"enabled to support current and next-generation GPUs, including NVIDIA Hopper and Blackwell"**

## 3. **NVIDIA Reference Architecture and Partner Status**
- *"IREN Cloud™ is built to NVIDIA reference architecture"* — meeting NVIDIA's own design standards for optimal performance
- Touting **NVIDIA Preferred Partner status** as a credential validating the quality of its cloud and infrastructure offerings

## 4. **High-Performance Networking**
- **Full non-blocking 3.2 TB/s InfiniBand connectivity** — critical for distributed AI training
- **Dual, physically diverse fiber paths** at virtually every facility
- **<6ms latency to network hubs** and high-bandwidth, low-latency interconnectivity (e.g., Oklahoma site)

## 5. **Advanced Liquid Cooling for Next-Gen GPUs**
- **Direct-to-chip, liquid-cooled AI data centers** (e.g., Childress facility)
- Positioning liquid cooling as a necessity for increasingly power-dense NVIDIA GPUs
- Highlighting that facilities are designed for **cutting-edge air and liquid cooling solutions**

## 6. **Speed to Market / Faster Time-to-Scale**
- *"Get your AI to market faster with advanced NVIDIA GPU clusters"*
- **"Faster time-to-scale"** explicitly listed as a business impact of next-generation NVIDIA systems
- First-mover speed implied in Blackwell deployment announcements

## 7. **Improved Cost Efficiency and Exclusive Pricing**
- **"Improved cost efficiency due to advances in technology"** with each new NVIDIA generation
- *"Deal directly with IREN's fully integrated data centers to access exclusive pricing"*
- Low-cost power (~**US$0.05/kWh** in West Texas), zero data egress fees, and vertically integrated operations reducing total cost of ownership

## 8. **Purpose-Built, High-Performance Compute Infrastructure**
- Data centers described as **"purpose-built for high-performance, power-dense compute"**
- Framing facilities as **"AI factories"** where GPUs are "workers" that are "clustered together, never clocking off, never tiring, able to scale to millions"
- **Max rack density of 70 kW** at multiple facilities
- Facilities marketed as **"AI-Ready"** and **"AI-Optimised"**

## 9. **Massive Power Capacity and Reliability**
- Power envelopes ranging from **30 MW to 2,000 MW** across sites, with up to **2.75 GW of grid-connected power** secured
- **Grid-connected** for continuous and reliable service with **on-site substations**
- Ability to secure critical infrastructure (e.g., bulk transformers) amid global supply constraints

## 10. **100% Renewable Energy and Sustainability**
- All facilities **powered by 100% renewable energy** (from clean/renewable sources or through RECs)
- Access to **abundant, low-cost renewable energy** (BC Hydro in British Columbia, wind/solar in West Texas)
- Leveraging stranded clean energy sources

## 11. **Deployment Flexibility and Choice**
- Offering customers **choice across multiple GPU tiers** simultaneously (H100, H200, B200, B300, GB300)
- Multiple service models: **AI Cloud, Colocation, and Build-to-Suit**
- Breadth of fleet (five NVIDIA GPU types at once) positioned as a competitive advantage

## 12. **Future-Proofed Infrastructure**
- Facilities **designed to accommodate the latest and upcoming NVIDIA architectures** without major overhauls
- Emphasis that infrastructure is ready **today and tomorrow**

## 13. **Detailed Hardware Specifications**
For each GPU offering, IREN provides concrete specs:
- **GPU Memory** (e.g., H100/80GB, H200/141GB)
- **RAM** (e.g., 2,048 GB)
- **vCPUs** (e.g., 224)
- **Storage** (e.g., 30TB NVMe)

## 14. **Tailored Customer Service**
- *"Customer service tailored to your GPU cloud needs"* — personalized support rather than a generic cloud experience
