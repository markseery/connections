## Years In Business

# Nebius — Years in Business

## Public Launch
- Nebius was publicly launched in **late 2023**. The two sources give slightly different months:
  - Batch 1 states **November 2023** (citing the company's own language: "Launched in November 2023").
  - Batch 2 states **October 2023** (noting the company celebrated its 1-year public-launch anniversary in October 2024).
  - **Best reconciliation:** The public launch likely occurred in **October–November 2023**. It is possible an initial announcement or soft launch happened in October with a formal public launch in November, or one source is slightly imprecise. The company itself appears to have marked approximately one year of operations in late 2024.

## First-Anniversary Milestones
- The company celebrated its **first anniversary** with a Nasdaq bell-ringing event in **Times Square** (Batch 1 places this in July 2025; Batch 2 references celebrating 1 year since public launch in October 2024). These may be two separate milestone events rather than a contradiction.

## Deeper Corporate History
- The broader corporate entity predates the Nebius brand significantly:
  - **Nebius Group N.V.** (the listed legal entity) held its **IPO in 2011** and is listed on **Nasdaq**.
  - Co-founder **Arkady Volozh** served as CEO from **2000 to 2022**, then resumed the role in **2024**.
  - **John Boynton** has been a Non-Executive Director since **2000**.
  - **Charles Ryan** became a director at the time of the 2011 IPO.
- This indicates the parent organization has roots going back **~25 years** (to 2000), with a public-market history of **~14 years** (since the 2011 IPO).

## Other Notable Dates
- Nebius completed a **full rewrite of its cloud platform** from the ground up in **October 2024**.

## Summary
Under the **Nebius** brand, the company has been operating publicly for roughly **1.5–2 years** (since late 2023). However, the underlying corporate entity has been in business since **2000** and publicly traded since **2011**, giving it a much longer institutional history.

# Consolidated Summary: Teraflops and Exaflops

## Key Findings

Neither batch provided extensive direct discussion of teraflops/exaflops as standalone metrics. However, several related performance and computational benchmarks were reported:

### Supercomputer Rankings & Efficiency
- **ISEG2 supercomputer**: Ranked **#13** on the Top500 list of world's fastest supercomputers; energy efficiency of **38.189 GFLOPs/watt** (#67 on Green500)
- **ISEG supercomputer** (predecessor): Previously ranked **#16**, later **#19** on Top500

### Training Compute Scale
- Training an LLM with **175 billion parameters** requires approximately **3.14×10²³ FLOPs** — illustrating the exaflop-scale compute demands of modern AI training

### NVIDIA System Performance
- **GB200 NVL72**: Delivers **130 TB/s** GPU-to-GPU communication bandwidth via NVLink 5
- **GB300 NVL72**: Delivers **1,000 tokens/second**, a **50× boost** in AI Factory output performance, and **30× improvement** for real-time video generation
- **MLPerf Training v5.1**: NVIDIA achieved **7 out of 9 first-place results** across HGX B300 and HGX B200 systems
  - **HGX B300** showed an average **12.6% reduction** in training time vs. HGX B200
- **MLPerf Llama 3.1 405B training**: **124.5 min** (128-node cluster) and **244.6 min** (64-node cluster), demonstrating **near-linear scaling** (1.97× speedup when doubling GPUs)

### Networking
- **InfiniBand**: Up to **3.2 Tbit/s** throughput per host
- **NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand**: Up to **800 Gbit/s** end-to-end connectivity

### Storage
- Shared filesystem: up to **100 GB/s** and **1M IOPS**; up to **1 TB/s** read throughput
- Object storage: **2 GB/s** per GPU

## Notes
- No batch provided explicit exaflops benchmarks (e.g., sustained exaflop/s figures for specific systems), though the training FLOPs estimate and Top500 rankings implicitly touch on exascale computing.
- The two batches were fully consistent with no contradictions; networking specs (3.2 Tbit/s InfiniBand, 800 Gbit/s Quantum-X800) appeared in both and were identical.
